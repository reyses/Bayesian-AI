2026-07-07, direct request: "it is much easier for me if you make shorter text
otherwise i latch on to something and dont read the rest so short sentences is better."

**Why:** Long / dense responses cause him to fixate on one early point and stop
reading the rest — so the important part (often the recommendation or question at
the end) gets missed. This has been visible all session: he responds to a fragment
of my message, not the whole thing.

**How to apply:** Short sentences. Short messages. Lead with the single most
important point. Put the ask/question where it can't be missed (short, near the top
or clearly at the end). One idea per line. Cut caveats to the essential one. Prefer a
2-4 line answer over a thorough one; if there's more, offer it ("want the detail?")
rather than dumping it. Pairs with [[report-distributions-and-mode]] (mode-first,
distributions) — same theme: lead with the essential, don't bury it.
