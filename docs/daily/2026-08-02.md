## 4. Honest interpretation

**The pre-committed verdict prints "fails on its own axis" and stands as the
gate result** — but the gate itself was mis-specified, and that is a finding:
with full-stop rates of 9–52%, the worst-5% bucket is saturated at −20.89 for
*every* arm, so **CVaR5's delta is identically zero by construction**. A tail
metric with α smaller than the stop-out frequency cannot see the tail change.
(Standing lesson: match the tail quantile to the stop rate, or use stop
frequency itself.)

What the un-gated, same-protocol numbers say:

1. **The mechanism does what the owner said it does.** Full stop-outs at his
   scale: **51.5% → 10.0%** — four of five catastrophic exits converted.
   Variance −73%. Losing-trade share −23pp.
2. **The guarantee is a cushion curve, not an absolute.** P(loss) falls
   monotonically 44% → 2.9% as the peak grows; at ≥20pt cushion the exit is
   near-armor (his live trade: MFE 38.5 → floor ~27). The leak is the
   single-bar collapse through the whole structure (stop-first ordering) —
   rare (10/1,053 at 1.5σ ≥20pt) but real, worst −20.89.
3. **Protection is not free at high frequency.** −0.26pt/trade × 100
   trades/day compounded into a **+45% deeper equity drawdown** at 1.5σ.
   Per-trade safety ≠ equity-curve safety. At his ~1–3 trades/day the cost
   term shrinks toward noise (3σ: maxDD −1%).
4. **Hysteresis (two-stage vs continuous 80%) is immaterial** — ±0.04pt
   either way. The freeze is philosophy, not P&L.

**Synthesis for the program:** the exit is a validated *variance/stop-frequency
machine* whose value depends on entry quality and trade frequency — worthless
under 100-trades/day mechanical entries (cost compounds), close to free and
massively tail-cutting under selective entries with real MFE. Which is exactly
the owner's architecture: *he* selects (the unsolved, corpus-bound part), the
mechanism keeps what the trade gives. Both halves now have numbers.

## 10. Adversarial verification — 3 agents vs the engine; 7 real flaws, all fixed

Per the commitment ("verification agents will attack it before it touches the
live tape"): three independent agents — fill-ordering/lookahead auditor,
BLIND reimplementation (spec + raw tape only, no engine source), and
persistence/state-machine auditor.

**Blind reimplementation: engine CONFIRMED.** All four scenario traces match
to the second and the tick (freeze 10:20:18/19654.90/+10.75; ratchet release
:55 → freeze :59/19642.30/+26.50; stop −9.14). The single 1s discrepancy on
the hard-exit scenario was my prompt's resume_ts, verified against the raw
:19 bar — both implementations apply the identical rule.

**Auditors: 7 real flaws found, all fixed and re-validated:**
1. **Deterministic crash in the NORMAL owner flow** — freeze-release cleared
   `frozen` but kept `prot_hard`; the next hard check computed None×0.7 →
   TypeError mid-bar (freeze → extend hard → price recovers = the standard
   sequence). Fix: hard is dormant while unfrozen (the ratchet); proven by
   synthetic unit test (`engine_unit_test.py` T-A).
2. **Engine ignored half the pos schema** — `call --target` NEVER filled
   under `run` (legacy `step` fills it: path-dependent trade semantics);
   `warnstop` levels were blown through silently; `stopreverse`/
   `bank_reenter` would book a plain stop. Fix: target = step 1.5 (resting
   limit at level; stop wins ties — opposite of legacy's optimistic
   tie-break, on purpose), warnstop = engine-native halt, reverse semantics
   = loud REFUSAL to run.
3. **step-after-run seam gap** — `step` popped `halt_ts5` then read it
   (always-stale fallback), and `_advance` skipped the halt-minute
   remainder entirely: a stop touched at :31 after a :07 halt never filled.
   Fix: `step` now consumes the remainder THROUGH the engine first (halt
   supersedes the step); validated — stop filled at :29 inside the
   remainder, +1.61 exact.
4. **Post-halt lookahead fills** — `call`/`exit` without `--at` filled at
   the full-minute close, up to ~59s of future vs the frozen frame (the
   9.75pt misprice class). Fix: default fill = 1s close AT the halt instant
   (`_halt_px`), explicit `--at` still wins.
5. **Gap-through stop = phantom fill** — the engine never read `open`;
   a bar gapping past the stop booked the level, a price the tape never
   printed (systematically optimistic; 1s gaps routine in MNQ). Fix: fill
   at open when gapped, labeled "(gapped past X)"; unit test T-B.
6. **Hard-exit close-fill optimism** — you cannot trade a price you just
   observed; fill moved to the NEXT second's open (trigger :19 close
   19658.25 → fill :20 open 19658.00 = +4.61 vs the old same-close +4.36).
7. **Region ping-pong + resume-replay class** — region cooldown/inside
   state was function-local (re-fired 1s after every halt while price dwelt
   in its own density band — `run` unusable exactly at decision zones);
   alarms re-fired while straddling; the run-after-legacy fallback replayed
   :56-:59 of committed bars (a fresh stop could fill on PRE-ENTRY wicks);
   `_eng_book` had a crash window between its two saves; milestones scored
   gap-bar peaks made after the deadline and mis-scored closed trades. All
   fixed: `region_state` persists in state, alarm edge-trigger (must leave
   and return), resume fallback +59, book+clock in ONE save (corpus line
   after), milestone uses pre-bar peak when late + last_peak/last_entry
   stash for closed trades. Plus: EOD leaves positions OPEN with a loud
   warning (never auto-flattens), protect-active banner on every new fill
   (standing arm=always config no longer silently protects a new trade),
   peek arithmetic clamped for engine stamps at :56-:59.

Full revalidation after fixes: T1/T2 freeze+hard exact, idempotent; T4
