# THE POCKET CARD — everything today settled, lizard-brain format
(2026-07-31 · every line has receipts in BRACKET_STRATEGY_VERDICT.md + the hazard tables)

## 1. ENTRIES ARE YOURS
Nothing mechanical earns entries. Every mechanical entry we tested lost.

## 2. THE FLOOR (always on)
Stop **−10** at entry → at **+10** move stop to **+2**.
Once armed you cannot lose. Verified: 18,766/18,766.

## 3. BREATHING ROOM (riding a move)
**20–25pt.** Never less — 10pt lives inside the noise
(the one-tick stop-out that cost the 290pt move).

## 4. FADING A LEVEL (limit at the level)
Different rule: stop **10–15pt past the level**. (+10 survives 98.5% of re-pokes.)

## 5. IS THIS PULLBACK A FAKEOUT? — three questions
| question | fine | worry |
|---|---|---|
| how DEEP? | <20% of the move | >50% |
| how LONG since last high? | <2 min | >3 min (>8 = done) |
| how FAST did it drop? | gradual grind | violent flush that stalls |

**shallow + short + slow = HOLD.  deep + stalled + fast = OUT.**

## 6. FAKEOUT COUNT = FUEL
Runners fake out 3–5×. Repeated fakeouts mean you're probably IN a winner.
The 3rd fakeout is when you HOLD, not when you brace.

## 7. INDICATORS ARE READINGS
Cubic, wick strip, the gauge — they describe, you decide.
Stops are fail-safes, not exit strategies.
