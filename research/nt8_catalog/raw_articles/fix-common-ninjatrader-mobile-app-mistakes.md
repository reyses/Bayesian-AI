# How to Solve 5 of the Most Common Mistakes on the NinjaTrader Mobile App

By
NinjaTrader Team

April 11, 2026

Getting started with the NinjaTrader mobile app is pretty straightforward; you log in, find familiar tools, and feel like you’re ready to roll. Then maybe something just isn’t where you expect it to be. Charts feel hidden, switching symbols isn’t obvious, and some features feel like they should be in certain places but aren’t.

If that sounds familiar, you’re not alone; plenty of users have issues transitioning from the desktop dashboard to mobile app. This guide will walk you through five of the most common sticking points and show you how to handle each one step by step, so you can spend less time searching and more time actually using the app to your advantage.

## **Mistake #1: Not knowing where to find the chart view**

On the NinjaTrader mobile app, charts aren’t hidden; they’re just one tap away in your Quotes view. You open a chart by tapping a market in your watchlist, not by going to a separate charts tab.

The Quotes view (the screen that shows your watchlist and live prices) is where everything starts. Once you know that, finding charts becomes second nature.

To get to the chart view:

1. Open the mobile app.
2. Go to **Quotes view**.
3. Look for the market you want to follow.
4. Tap it to open the chart.

For example, if you see ES in your list, just tap it and the chart appears. No extra navigation needed.

Once this clicks, the rest of the NinjaTrader mobile charting experience tends to feel much more intuitive.

## **Mistake #2: Not knowing how to switch between instruments**

To switch instruments on the NinjaTrader mobile app, tap the search icon inside the chart and type a symbol like MNQ or ES. It’s quick once you know where to look.

The NinjaTrader mobile app is designed to keep you in the chart while you move between markets. That way, you’re not constantly backing out and starting over.

To switch between instruments:

1. Open any chart in the mobile app.
2. Tap the **search icon** at the top.
3. Enter the symbol you want (e.g., MNQ).
4. Select it from the results.
5. The chart updates automatically.

Let’s say you’re watching ES but want to check the MNQ. Type **MNQ**, tap it, and you’re there.

This is one of those NinjaTrader mobile app tips that can save you time every session once it becomes a habit.

## **Mistake #3: Not knowing how to scroll or zoom on a chart**

On the NinjaTrader mobile app, you navigate charts using simple gestures—pinch to zoom and swipe to scroll. There are no buttons for this, which can feel unfamiliar at first.

Key takeaway

If you can pinch and swipe, you already know how to navigate a NinjaTrader mobile chart.

Once you get used to it, though, it can be a smooth way to explore price action:

1. Open a chart in the mobile app.
2. Use two fingers to **pinch out** to zoom in.
3. Pinch in to zoom back out.
4. Swipe left or right to move through time.
5. Adjust until you’re focused on the area you want.

For example, if you want a closer look at a recent move in NQ, just zoom in on that section and swipe to see what led up to it.

These gestures are a big part of how NinjaTrader mobile charting works. After a bit of practice, they can start to feel more natural.

## **Mistake #4: Trying to add drawing tools**

Drawing tools aren’t currently available on the NinjaTrader mobile app. If you’re trying to add trendlines or mark levels, you’re not missing anything; they’re just not part of the mobile feature set.

This catches a lot of traders off guard, especially if they’re used to drawing directly on charts from a desktop setup.

To add drawing tools, you’ll need to hop outside the mobile app:

1. Identify the drawing tool you’re looking for (trendlines, support/resistance, etc.).
2. Switch to the **NinjaTrader desktop or web platform** for that task.
3. Go back to the mobile app to monitor, manage trades, and review indicators.

For example, you might mark key levels on ES from your desktop before the session, then use mobile to keep an eye on price throughout the day.

**If this is a feature you can’t live without,**drawing tools are currently available on the NinjaTrader desktop and web platforms.

Knowing this upfront can save time and help you choose the right platform for each part of your workflow.

## **Mistake #5: Not knowing how to add or configure indicators**

You can add indicators on the NinjaTrader mobile app by tapping the Indicator button beneath the chart. That opens a menu with built-in tools you can apply right away.

To add or configure indicators:

1. Open a chart in the mobile app.
2. Tap the **Indicator button** (the control beneath the chart).
3. Browse or select an indicator.
4. Adjust the settings if needed.
5. Apply it to your chart.

For example, you might add a momentum indicator or moving average to get more context around ES price action.

Once you know where this menu lives, it becomes a quick way to customize your charts without leaving the screen.

## **FAQs about the NinjaTrader mobile app**

A few questions come up consistently when traders are learning how to use NinjaTrader mobile. Here are some quick answers.

### **Why can’t I find the chart button on NinjaTrader mobile?**

There isn’t a separate chart button. In the NinjaTrader mobile app, charts open when you tap an instrument in Quotes view.

### **How do I zoom in on a NinjaTrader mobile chart?**

Use a pinch-out gesture with two fingers to zoom in, and pinch in to zoom out. Swipe to scroll through price history.

### **Does NinjaTrader mobile support drawing tools?**

No. Drawing tools are not currently part of the NinjaTrader mobile app; you’ll need the desktop or web platform for that.

### **How do I change symbols on a chart?**

Tap the search icon from within the chart and type the symbol you want, like MNQ or ES.

### **What is the SuperDOM on NinjaTrader mobile?**

The SuperDOM (a price ladder used for order entry and market depth) is a different view focused on placing and managing trades rather than charting.
