# Mastering Entry and Exit Strategies in Futures Trading

By
NinjaTrader Team

June 26, 2025

When it comes to trading futures, having a strong grasp of entry and exit strategies can be just as important as understanding market trends and technical indicators. Entry and exit futures trading strategies are key components of a well-rounded trading plan, helping traders identify when to get in—and out—of trades with purpose and discipline.

Let’s explore what entry and exit strategies are, why they’re essential for futures traders, and how they can support more consistent decision-making in the markets.

## What are entry and exit strategies?

An entry strategy defines the conditions under which you open a futures position. These conditions can be based on technical indicators (e.g., moving averages, RSI, MACD), fundamental analysis, [chart patterns](/learn/technical-analysis/identify-chart-patterns-on-futures-trading-charts/), or even time-based rules.

An exit strategy outlines how and when you will close a position. This includes both profit-taking exits and risk management exits, such as stop-loss orders or trailing stops. Together, these strategies create a roadmap that can guide your trades from start to finish.

## Why traders use entry and exit strategies

Without a clear plan, it’s easy for emotions like fear and greed to influence trading decisions. Entry and exit strategies can help bring structure to your process by:

* **Reducing guesswork:** Predefined conditions provide clarity in volatile markets
* **Supporting risk management:**Strategic exits help protect capital and limit losses. Learn how to [define your risk management strategy](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/).
* **Promoting consistency:** Using the same approach over time can help you evaluate and refine your strategy
* **Avoiding emotional trades:** Following a set plan can help you reduce any impulsive decisions. Learn how to [build a futures trading plan](/futures/futures-trading-basics/futures-trading-plan/).

## Types of entry strategies in futures trading

Entry strategies in futures trading vary based on the trader’s style and market conditions. Some common approaches include:

* **Breakout entries:** Entering a trade when price breaks above resistance or below support levels
* **Pullback entries:** Waiting for a brief retracement before entering in the direction of the trend
* **Indicator-based entries:** Using tools like [MACD](/futures/blogs/what-is-the-macd-indicator/), [Bollinger Bands](/futures/blogs/bollinger-bands-explained/), or volume to time entries

Each method has its strengths and weaknesses. The key is selecting futures trading strategies that align with your risk tolerance and trading goals.

## Types of exit strategies in futures trading

Exit strategies in futures trading are essential for managing risk and locking in gains. A few widely used methods include:

* **Stop-loss orders:** Automatically close a trade at a predetermined loss level. Learn about [types of stop loss orders and when to use them](/futures/blogs/stop-loss-orders-which-order-type-to-use/).
* **Take-profit targets:**Close trades once a specific profit level is reached
* **Trailing stops:** Adjust stop-loss levels as the market moves in your favor, locking in gains while giving room for growth

Combining different types of exits—e.g., using both a stop-loss and a take-profit order—can help traders balance risk and reward more effectively.

## How entry and exit strategies can improve your trading

Using well-defined entry and exit strategies can benefit futures traders in several ways:

* **Sharper decision-making:**Knowing your exact criteria for entering and exiting a trade keeps your focus on the market, not your emotions.
* **Greater discipline:** Having a set plan can help you stay committed to your strategy even during periods of volatility.
* **Improved performance tracking:**When you follow a consistent plan, it's easier to evaluate what’s working and make data-driven adjustments.

While no strategy guarantees success, disciplined entry and exit planning can give you a stronger foundation in your trading journey.

## Start with a plan and refine it over time

Effective futures trading doesn’t come from spontaneous decisions—it’s built on strategy and self-discipline. Entry and exit futures trading strategies can help you define that path with clarity and confidence.

If you're just beginning your futures trading journey, start small. Experiment with a strategy in a simulated environment, track your performance, and refine your approach as you go. With time, your entry and exit strategies can evolve into one of the most valuable tools in your trading toolkit.
