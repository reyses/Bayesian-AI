# Is the Fed Rate Hike Over? With Dan Gramza

By
NinjaTrader Team

October 19, 2023

The next Federal Open Market Committee meeting on November 1 is approaching fast. The conversation around the ultimate terminal rate for the federal funds target range is still contentious. This week is chock-full of Fed officials’ speaking engagements. Dan Gramza, owner of Gramza Capital Management, joined us on “See the Futures” this week for an in-depth discussion around the Fed rate hike. Is it over? Also, a discussion of US monetary policy past, present, and potential future actions.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Why the Fed may have one rate hike left to fight inflation
* Yield curve inversion as a recession indicator
* Subjectivity of a 2% inflation rate target
* Why the Russell 2000 could be a barometer for the US economy
* Chart analysis of E-mini S&P 500 futures, E-mini Russell 2000 futures, and 2-year, 10-year and 30-year futures
