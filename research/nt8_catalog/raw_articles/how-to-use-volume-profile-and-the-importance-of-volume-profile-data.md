# How to Use Volume Profile and the Importance of Volume Profile Data

By
NinjaTrader Team

June 27, 2025

Volume isn’t just a measure of how much is being traded—it can also reveal where key activity is taking place. For active futures traders, understanding volume profile data can offer a clearer picture of the market’s structure and help pinpoint areas of interest like support, resistance, and potential breakout zones.

If you’re looking to improve your volume profile trading strategy, gaining a deeper understanding of how this data works—and how to read it on your charts—can be a valuable step in your development as a trader.

## What is volume profile?

Volume profile is a charting technique that displays the total trading volume at each price level for a specified period. Unlike a traditional volume histogram that shows activity over time, volume profile runs horizontally along the price axis, showing where the most buying and selling occurred.

This allows traders to identify high- and low-volume areas, which often align with support and resistance zones. A key concept in volume profile is the point of control (POC)—the price level with the highest volume traded during the session or time range.

## Why volume profile matters in futures trading

Futures markets can be fast-paced and filled with [volatility](/learn/technical-analysis/use-volatility-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/). Having insight into where significant buying and selling is taking place can help you:

* Determine price levels where markets may pause or reverse
* Validate trends or confirm breakouts
* Anticipate areas of support and resistance more clearly
* Make more informed entry and exit decisions

In volume profile trading, many participants look for volume nodes:

* **High-volume nodes (HVNs)**often indicate price areas of consolidation or acceptance
* **Low-volume nodes (LVNs)** suggest areas where price may move quickly, with less resistance

These zones can help traders spot potential turning points and define risk more effectively.

By using volume profile in this way, you're basing your decision on where the market has shown interest—not just price action alone.

## Benefits of using volume profile in your trading strategy

Incorporating volume profile data into your routine can offer several advantages, including:

* **Market clarity:** See where the bulk of trading is happening, not just price movement.
* **Strategic entries and exits:** Use data-driven levels to plan your trades.
* **Increased confidence:** Validate setups with clear volume-based context.
* **Flexibility:** Apply to short-term scalps or longer-term swing setups.

Whether you’re new to futures or looking to refine your approach, volume profile can help you build a trading strategy rooted in observable market behavior.
