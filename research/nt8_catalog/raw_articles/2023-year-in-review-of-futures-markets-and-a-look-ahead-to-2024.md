# 2023 Year in Review of Futures Markets and A Look Ahead to 2024

By
NinjaTrader Team

December 21, 2023

Since futures markets allow traders to participate in all major asset classes, there's no better way to end the year than by looking back and ahead at some of the major CME Group futures markets. What fundamental and technical factors have affected price behavior in 2023, and how might 2024 play out? TJM Institutional Services Managing Director Jim Iuorio spoke with NinjaTrader’s Jim Cagnina about possible paths ahead for multiple assets in the coming year.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Three reasons for [Bitcoin’s](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/cryptocurrency/bitcoin/) bullish tendency in 2023
* Discussion on central banks’ influence on the price of [gold futures](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/metals/gold/)
* Opinion on why the 10-Year Yield is not behaving as expected
* Explanation of why we are all rates traders, whether we know it or not
* Chart analysis of [Micro Nasdaq](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/index/e-mini-nasdaq/), gold, [crude oil](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/energy/crude-oil/), British pound, Euro FX, Bitcoin, and 10-Year Note futures
* Comparison chart of 2-Year, 10-Year, and 30-Year Yield futures
