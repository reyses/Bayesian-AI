# Trade Smarter in 2025: Five New Year’s Resolutions for Futures Trading

By
NinjaTrader Team

January 09, 2025

As we glide into a new year of trading, it’s a great time to reflect, refocus, and set goals for personal growth and motivation. As futures traders, we all know the markets may be unpredictable, volatile, and frustrating, but a commitment to self-improvement can set us off on the right path. Trading isn’t just about charts and indicators—it’s about a mindset that includes discipline, adaptation, and a commitment to always be learning.

This year, why not set some resolutions that can help improve your focus and make you a more consistent trader? Here are five suggested New Year’s resolutions tailored for futures traders, designed to help you sharpen your skills and tackle 2025 with confidence.

## 1. I Will Learn From Other Traders

Trading can often feel like a solo journey, but it doesn’t have to be one. Resolve to connect with other traders this year. Join a Discord channel, attend more webinars, and participate in live trading events. Watching how others approach the markets, handle risk, and adapt to changing conditions can open your eyes to new possibilities.

Commit to following one or two traders you admire and make them your mentor (officially or unofficially), whether on social media or through a community. And don’t just focus on their wins—learn from their losses as well. Trading is all about collaboration, so share your ideas and methods with other traders, too.

## 2. I Will Get My Emotions Under Control

If you’ve ever chased a loss or hesitated to take a trade, you know how emotions can wreak havoc on your performance. This year, focus on keeping those emotions in check. [Start a trading journal](/futures/blogs/trading-journal-log-your-trades-with-ninjatrader%E2%80%99s-journal-display/) to document your mindset during trades and pinpoint when emotions get the best of you.

Consider adopting pretrade checklists to ensure your decisions are logical, not reactive. If you find yourself overwhelmed, mindfulness practices like deep breathing or even stepping away from the screen can make a huge difference.

## 3. I Will Master Three New Trade Setups

The markets are constantly evolving, and so should your playbook. Commit to mastering three new trade setups this year. Maybe it’s learning to trade breakouts, perfecting pullback entries, or using advanced tools like VWAP.

Visually backtest each idea, understand how each setup performs in different market conditions, then slowly integrate them into your live trading. Expanding your arsenal of setups will help ensure you’ll be ready for whatever the market throws at you.

## 4. I Will Apply a Consistent Risk Management Approach

You may already have a [risk management plan](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) in place, but it’s likely that it could be better and you could be more consistent and disciplined. Remember, risk management is non-negotiable. This year, make it your mission to have a clear, appropriate risk management plan for your account and every trade you make. Define your maximum loss per trade, stick to position sizing rules, and know your overall account risk limits.

Here’s the challenge: Write your risk management rules down and treat them like a contract with yourself. Revisiting these rules regularly will reinforce discipline and help protect your account from preventable drawdowns.

## 5. I Will Review and Optimize My Trading Routine Every Month

Routines are the backbone of trading success, but even the best routines need refining. Set aside time at the end of each month to review your performance and assess your habits. Are you sticking to your trading plan? Are you preparing adequately for the trading day? Are there recurring mistakes you should address?

Use these monthly reviews to make small, meaningful adjustments to your daily workflow. Over time, these tweaks will compound, helping you trade more efficiently and effectively.

## Looking Ahead Into 2025

Resolutions aren’t about perfection; they’re about steady progress. By setting realistic, actionable trading goals, you can create habits that drive long-term success. Remember, the markets will always present challenges—but with the right mindset and tools, you can tackle them head-on.

Every new year is a fresh start, so here’s to a year of learning, growth, and new opportunities. And whatever resolutions you’re setting for yourself this year, stick with them, and make 2025 a fantastic trading year!
