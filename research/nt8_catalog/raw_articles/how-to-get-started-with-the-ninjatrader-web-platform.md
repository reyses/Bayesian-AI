# Platforms Unleashed: Getting Started With the NinjaTrader Web Platform

By
NinjaTrader Team

June 27, 2025

As futures trading evolves, so does the way traders connect to markets. Whether you're at home or on the move, the NinjaTrader Web platform brings flexible, browser-based trading directly to your screen—no downloads required.

In this guide, we’ll introduce you to the platform and walk through how to set up, customize, and navigate the platform with confidence.

## What is NinjaTrader Web?

NinjaTrader Web is the cloud-based version of the [NinjaTrader platform](/trading-platform/), accessible via browser on desktop or laptop. It delivers a streamlined trading experience that syncs with your account, allowing you to monitor positions, execute trades, and conduct analysis anywhere you have internet access.

You can trade directly from charts, customize your workspace layout, and access many of the features found in NinjaTrader Desktop—all in your browser window.

## Key components of the platform

Here’s a look at the main elements of the platform and how to use them.

### Modular layout

NinjaTrader Web is built around a modular system. You can add, remove, and move modules like:

* Chart
* Trading ladder (DOM)
* Quote board
* Orders and positions
* Account info and margin reports

Each module can be resized, reorganized, or set to full-screen, making it easy to design a workspace that fits your trading style.

To add a new module, click the “+ Add Module” button in the top-left corner, choose what you’d like to add (e.g., a new chart), and position it how you like.

![NinjaTrader Web platform showing modular layout](/getmedia/401492a6-5b12-4579-af3c-48088a224d6b/WebTrader_Add_Modules.jpg)Figure 1: NinjaTrader Web platform showing modular layout with market analyzer, chart, and orders windows with a drag & drop to dashboard feature. 

## Trading tools at your fingertips

The platform gives you several ways to place and manage trades.

#### Trading ladder (DOM)

![Highlighted view of the DOM](/getmedia/45f6042f-78de-4775-b86c-8a228f7e6f4b/WebTrader_DOM.jpg)Figure 2: Highlighted view of the DOM (Depth of Market) module in NinjaTrader web platform. 

The depth of market (DOM) module lets you view market depth, place trades, and manage orders. You can:

* Place limit or stop orders with a single click.
* Use drag-and-drop to adjust working orders.
* View real-time estimated P&L.
* Set up automated trade management (ATM) strategies.

### Chart trading

![Chart view in NinjaTrader](/getmedia/eba8e771-fd82-4327-8a75-7f7fda6d54df/WebTrader_Chart_Trade_1.jpg)Figure 3: Chart view in NinjaTrader with Trade Mode enabled, showing active trades, indicators, and drawing tools. Want to trade directly from your chart? Enable Trade Mode in the chart settings to place and manage trades without leaving the chart view. You can also:

* Use drawing tools to identify trends and levels.
* Add indicators like RSI, Bollinger Bands, or moving averages.
* Zoom in on specific timeframes or customize chart styles.

***Learn More:*** [how to trade from the charts using NinjaTrader chart trader](/futures/blogs/how-to-use-ninjatrader-chart-trader).

## Real-time account management

![NinjaTrader Account Management](/getmedia/6023d31d-f8cb-4041-b0f0-0ce3f4b2df6c/WebTrader_Account_Management.jpg)Figure 4: Top navigation bar in NinjaTrader Web Platform. From the top bar of the platform, you can quickly access key account information:

* **Account dropdown****:**Switch between sim and live accounts

* **Open P&L****:** Monitor your real-time trade performance

* **Margin report****:** See how much margin is used and available

This can help you stay on top of your capital and risk, whether you’re actively trading or monitoring positions.

## Customizing your workspace

The NinjaTrader Web platform is highly customizable. Here are some features you can adjust:

* **Appearance**: Switch between light and dark themes.

* **Time settings****:** Set your local time zone.

* **Notifications****:**Get alerts by email or push notifications.

* **Chart layouts****:**Save your workspace and reuse across sessions.

You can also add or hide columns in your quote board and order history to show only the information that matters to you.

## Viewing your performance and trade history

![Performance reports screen](/getmedia/21f9a7b6-714b-4ea4-a020-edd0baf4510a/WebTrader_Account_Performance.jpg)Figure 5: "Performance reports screen in NinjaTrader Web showing all trades, profit trades, and P&L history. The built-in Reports section provides powerful insights into your trading:

* **Performance summary****:** Track win rates, average trade duration, and more.

* **Order history****:**Review trades across multiple contracts and dates.

* **Account balance history****:**Monitor changes to your account equity over time.

These tools can help you analyze your trading performance and refine your strategies.

## Why choose NinjaTrader Web?

The platform is ideal for traders who want:

* **Browser-based access****:** No downloads or installations needed.

* **Platform synchronization****:** Your data syncs across NinjaTrader Desktop, Web, and Mobile.

* **On-the-go flexibility****:**Trade from anywhere with an internet connection.

Whether you prefer the simplicity of web-based trading or need a backup platform while traveling, NinjaTrader Web offers a robust and intuitive solution.
