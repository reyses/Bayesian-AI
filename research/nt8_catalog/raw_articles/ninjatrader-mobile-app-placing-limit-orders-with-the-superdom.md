# Setting the Standard for Futures Trading on the Go With the NinjaTrader Mobile App: Placing Limit Orders With the SuperDOM

By
NinjaTrader Team

May 05, 2025

When you're ready to elevate your [futures trading strategy](/futures/futures-trading-basics/futures-trading-plan/) on the go, the SuperDOM in the NinjaTrader mobile app is your secret weapon. With just a few taps, you can place precise limit and stop orders using a sleek, intuitive interface—right from your mobile device.

## Getting started with SuperDOM

Open the [NinjaTrader mobile app](/trading-platform/mobile-trading/) and head to the Quotes view. Tap the market you're interested in (e.g., U.S. Treasury bonds) to open its chart. At the top of the screen, you’ll find the SuperDOM option—your gateway to fast and flexible order placement.

## Configuring your trade with SuperDOM

Before you place an order, tap the Trade button just below the SuperDOM. Here, you can:

* Set your order quantity
* Activate bracket orders for automatic profit-taking and [stop-loss levels](/futures/blogs/stop-loss-orders-which-order-type-to-use/)
* Enable a trailing stop to lock in gains

Once your preferences are dialed in, you’re ready to trade.

#### Placing a limit order

To place a buy limit order:

1. Choose your price on the SuperDOM by holding your finger on the desired level below the current market price (e.g., 111'04).
2. Look for the button that will appear at the bottom (e.g., Buy 1 Limit at 111'04).
3. Tap it, and your order is live.

Want to sell? Use the same method to place a sell limit order—just press and release at the price you want that’s above the current market price, then confirm your order.

#### Modifying and canceling orders with Ease in SuperDOM

Orders you place in the SuperDOM aren’t set in stone. You can:

* Drag the order label to a new price
* Tap the arrow to adjust the order one tick at a time
* Cancel with the X icon or use the Close button to clear all active orders

#### Placing a stop order

Stop orders are just as easy to place. To place a buy stop, pick a price above the market, hold your finger at that price on the opposite side of the liquidity histogram, and tap the confirmation button. To place a sell stop, pick a price below the market, hold your finger at that price on the opposite side of the liquidity histogram, and tap the confirmation button.
