# Technical Analysis Explained: Identifying Channels

By
NinjaTrader Team

July 21, 2025

Understanding price movements is essential for making informed decisions in futures trading. One effective method for interpreting these movements is through trend channels. These charting tools can help futures traders visually identify areas of support and resistance, spot potential breakouts, and determine directional bias—without relying on complex calculations.

In this post, we’ll explore what trend channels are, how they’re drawn, and how they can support your technical analysis strategy.

## What are trend channels?

Trend channels are visual tools that frame price action within parallel lines, representing consistent upward, downward, or sideways movement over a specific period. They consist of two lines:

* A primary trendline drawn along either the highs or lows
* A secondary parallel line on the opposite side of the price action

These lines together form a “channel” that can help define the market’s behavior and provide insights into potential future movements.

## Drawing a basic trend channel

To draw a downward trend channel:

1. Identify the start of a recent price move downward.
2. Connect the initial peak with a subsequent lower high to draw the top boundary (resistance).
3. Create a parallel line that touches the lowest low between those two highs to form the bottom boundary (support).

This method doesn’t require any advanced formulas; it’s about selecting visually significant high and low points on your chart. The Trend Channel tool on the NinjaTrader platform enables you to draw the channel you see with just three clicks of the mouse.

## How trend channels support decision-making

Once drawn, trend channels can act as guides for:

* **Support and resistance levels:** These can help identify where price may stall or reverse. Learn how to [identify intraday support and resistance levels](/futures/blogs/how-to-identify-intraday-support-and-resistance-levels-in-your-trading/).
* **Order placement:** Traders might use the upper boundary for potential short entries and the lower boundary for potential long entries, if other indicators align.
* **Stop and target setting:** Channels can help frame risk and reward by highlighting expected price boundaries.

For example, in a downtrending market like the Nasdaq post-July peak, traders might anticipate price reactions near the upper and lower bounds of the channel. A rejection near the upper channel could signal a continuation of the downtrend, while a break above might suggest a trend reversal.

## Applying channels to intraday charts

Trend channels aren’t limited to daily charts. Intraday traders can apply them to shorter time frames—like 5- or 10-minute charts—to capture smaller price moves throughout the trading day. The same process applies: define the trend direction, mark significant highs or lows, and draw your channel accordingly.

Even in sideways markets, channels can frame price oscillations and help guide entry and exit points.

## Exploring Donchian and Keltner channels

Beyond manually drawn channels, indicators like Donchian and Keltner channels offer a more automated approach:

* **Donchian channels** plot the highest high and lowest low over a set period (e.g., 20 bars). These can highlight breakout opportunities, particularly when prices push beyond the channel’s boundaries.
* **Keltner channels** use an exponential moving average as the midline and offset the upper and lower bands using average true range (ATR). They can help measure volatility and identify trends when price consistently tests or closes outside the bands.

These tools serve a similar purpose to manually drawn channels—framing price behavior—but rely on built-in calculations rather than visual interpretation.

## Why do channels work?

One theory behind the effectiveness of trend channels is that many traders use them, which reinforces their impact on price behavior. When enough participants recognize a trendline or channel boundary, it can influence collective action, creating support or resistance.

Still, it’s important to remember that channels are guides, not guarantees. They can break when new information or shifting market sentiment alters the prevailing trend. You should always use channels in conjunction with other indicators or strategies.

## Using trend channels in your trading

Adding trend channels to your analysis toolkit can offer clarity and structure, especially when navigating volatile futures markets. They can help traders visualize [price momentum](/learn/technical-analysis/momentum-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/) and identify trading zones, making them a valuable component in both swing and intraday strategies.
