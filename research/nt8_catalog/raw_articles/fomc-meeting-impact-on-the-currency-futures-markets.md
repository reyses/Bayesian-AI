By
NinjaTrader Team

July 27, 2023

The Federal Reserve Board's July FOMC meeting results are imminent. With a 25-basis point hike thought to be baked in the cake, the impact of the Fed's monetary policy actions is far from certain.    
  
Special guest Stephen Jonathan, a long-term market professional, joined a recent episode of “See the Futures” to discuss market outlooks. As Stephen is notably versed in currency markets, the focus is on CME Group currency futures markets in light of FOMC policy and other international forces.  
  
  
  
**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Commitment of Traders for currency traders
* Different global uses for the US dollar
* Level of coordination between central banks
* Federal Reserve meeting minutes to understand forward guidance
* Chart analysis of Japanese Yen futures
