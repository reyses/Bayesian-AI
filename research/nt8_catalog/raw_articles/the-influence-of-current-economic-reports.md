# The Influence of Current Economic Reports

By
NinjaTrader

![STF-3-2-23.png](/getattachment/d31033a6-2eaa-4b15-a578-30404e3658c9/STF-3-2-23.png "STF-3-2-23.png")  
  
As we hit the halfway mark between FOMC meetings, economic reports such as International Trade in Goods, domestic retail inventories, and domestic wholesale inventories can influence market participants’ outlook as to what the Fed will do next. We discuss the data's possible impact on the course of the US Dollar, Treasury Yields and ultimately Federal Reserve monetary policy.

#### How Current Economic Trends May Affect Your Trading Decisions

We discuss why the current economic trend is disinflationary (but not deflationary). Our experts  
continue to break down the current CPI and retail sales report and explain how consumer spending and saving trends are not reflecting a deflationary environment.

**Additional topics covered in the livestream include:**

* A discussion on the severity and importance of 2-year/10-year yield inversion
* An discussion of multiple indications of recession probability
* An opinion on the delayed effect of rate hikes
* A look at the relationship of the S&P 500 vs. 2-year yield
* Charting and price analysis of E-Mini S&P 500, Gold, Copper and Bitcoin
