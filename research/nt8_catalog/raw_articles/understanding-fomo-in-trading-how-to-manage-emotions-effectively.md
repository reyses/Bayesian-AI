# What is FOMO in trading?

By
NinjaTrader Team

November 07, 2024

The fear of missing out (FOMO) is a powerful psychological reaction that traders experience that can lead to poor decision-making. It occurs when traders feel anxious about missing out on a recent profitable trading opportunity, causing them to make impulsive, emotion-driven decisions.

## The dangers of FOMO trading

FOMO can cause you to chase trades, overtrade, and make discretionary trades that are inconsistent with your normal trading plan. You might take on more risk or jump into and out of multiple trades without proper analysis, all of which can result in significant monetary losses and loss of confidence. These types of emotional traps break the disciplined mindset required for long-term success in futures trading.

## 3 Ways to Mitigate FOMO Trading

The best way to mitigate FOMO when trading is to set clear boundaries and keep your long-term goals in mind. Here are three expert tips to help you succeed.

1. **Develop and stick to a trading plan:** A solid [trading plan](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-trading-basics/futures-trading-plan/) includes clear entry and exit rules, risk management strategies, and defined goals. Sticking to your plan can help you avoid the urge to jump into a trade just because there seems to be a new opportunity, reducing the emotional pressure of FOMO.
2. **Focus on long-term success:** Shifting the focus from short-term gains to long-term growth and consistency can help you view any missed trades as part of the trading process. Not every opportunity has to be captured to succeed. Learning to accept that there will always be another trade fosters patience and diminishes FOMO.
3. **Limit exposure to external noise:** Constantly checking social media, market news, or other traders’ opinions can amplify your FOMO. By reducing exposure to these external sources of pressure and instead focusing on personal analysis and strategy, you can make trading decisions based on rational thought rather than emotional reactions.

## Prevent FOMO Trading With Emotional Discipline

Trading requires more than just technical skills and knowledge—it demands emotional awareness and experience dealing with emotional challenges. How you react to emotions like fear, greed, and excitement are critical, as FOMO can cloud your judgment and lead to impulsive actions that can derail a solid trading plan.

Traders must learn to recognize and manage their emotions, staying mindful of how their feelings influence their decisions. Emotional discipline allows traders to make better objective choices based on their trading plan rather than reacting to market noise or unsupported personal biases.
