# Introducing the All-New NinjaTrader Dashboard

By
NinjaTrader Team

March 19, 2026

Your new NinjaTrader dashboard is here, and it’s built for how you trade!

This isn’t just an updated account dashboard. It’s a connected trading hub designed to support your growth as a trader. From real-time market breakdowns to structured learning paths, the NinjaTrader new dashboard brings education and community directly into your daily workflow—right alongside your account tools and performance data.

Key takeaway

It’s one dashboard, fully connected, designed to support how you trade today and tomorrow.

## Built-in education and live market access

With the new NinjaTrader dashboard, everything’s where you expect it to be—so you can spend less time navigating and more time focused on the markets.

### Get daily guidance with NinjaTrader Live

Markets move fast. Preparation and context matter. With NinjaTrader Live built directly into your dashboard, you can tune in each trading day and see how seasoned pros approach the session in real time.

* See how they prepare before the open.
* Watch where they engage the market.
* Learn how they approach risk management.
* Ask questions as the action unfolds.

Follow along as setups develop. Hear the thought process behind key decisions. Gain perspective you can apply to your own trading plan.

It’s real-time market context and professional insight, available right in your dashboard every trading day.

### Find your community

Trading is personal, but it doesn’t have to be isolated. Your new dashboard features access to a curated lineup of credible day trading leaders hosting live trading rooms, giving you the opportunity to learn alongside others who are actively engaged in the markets.

* Explore different strategies and perspectives.
* Connect with traders who share your goals.
* Discover an approach that aligns with your style.

Watch how others think through setups. See how they respond to changing conditions. Engage in real-time conversations that add context to your own decision-making.

Trading can be a solo endeavor, but surrounding yourself with the right community can help you stay sharp and accountable as the markets evolve.

![Introducing the All-New NinjaTrader Dashboard - Community](/getmedia/cae2c71a-ab06-4363-93bb-d3e614b01b17/Introducing-the-All-New-NinjaTrader-Dashboard-Community.png)*Figure 1: Community*

### Master it at your own pace

Every trader’s journey looks different. Whether you’re just getting started or refining your edge, your new dashboard includes on-demand education designed to grow with you. In it, you’ll find:

* Structured courses and bite-sized lessons
* On-demand content from experienced traders
* Educational resources that continue to expand based on community feedback

Start with the fundamentals or jump straight into advanced topics. Explore new concepts. Revisit lessons that matter most to your strategy.

Learn when it fits your schedule. Move at your own speed. Build your knowledge over time, one insight at a time.

Together, these features make the NinjaTrader new dashboard more than a place to manage your account; it becomes part of how you prepare, learn, and evolve as a trader.

![Introducing the All-New NinjaTrader Dashboard - Settings](/getmedia/d24f1131-371a-4d90-b4f3-1f06a18c61b5/Introducing-the-All-New-NinjaTrader-Dashboard-Settings.png)*Figure 2: Account Settings*

## Stay close to your performance

Your performance data shouldn’t live in a separate tool or require extra steps to review. With enhanced performance charts built directly into your dashboard, your trading activity is always within reach.

See the numbers that matter, right when you log in:

* Track your performance at a glance.
* Review your consistency over time.
* Identify areas where you’re getting sharper.
* Spot patterns that can help inform your next move.

Drill into your results. Step back and look at trends. Use your data to evaluate what’s working and where adjustments may be needed.

When your performance insights live alongside your account tools, education, and live market access, everything connects. You need clarity in your data and context around your decisions. Now both are built in, so you can focus on developing your edge.

![Introducing the All-New NinjaTrader Dashboard - Trading Performance Insights](/getmedia/22facee5-0a15-456c-89ad-5e9e72657e32/Introducing-the-All-New-NinjaTrader-Dashboard-Trading-Performance-Insights.png)*Figure 3: Trading Performance Insights*

## We’re just getting started

The move to a single, unified NinjaTrader dashboard marks the next phase of the platform’s evolution.

By consolidating tools, education, performance tracking, and market access into a single experience, we’re building a stronger foundation for what comes next. More insights. More expert guidance. More ways to sharpen your edge.

The new NinjaTrader dashboard is designed to grow with you. As your strategy evolves and your goals shift, your workspace evolves right alongside it.

Key takeaway

One login. One hub. Built for how you trade today—and where you want to take it next.
