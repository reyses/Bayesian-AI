# Trading Futures During Periods of High Market Volatility

By
NinjaTrader Team

July 01, 2024

In times of uncertainty, the futures markets can present opportunities for traders to capitalize on market volatility. With the right strategies and [risk management](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) techniques, you can navigate the ups and downs of volatile markets and take advantage of these opportunities.

## How to trade futures during volatile markets: a guide for beginners

Trading futures during periods of [high market volatility](/learn/technical-analysis/use-volatility-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/) can be a daunting prospect with many pitfalls, but with the right knowledge and approach, it can provide actionable opportunities. As leveraged instruments, futures contracts give traders the opportunity to control a larger position with a relatively smaller amount of capital. This may magnify profits during periods of high volatility but may also lead to significant losses.

Set yourself up with these three must-haves for futures trading:

### 1. Define your risk management strategy

Futures traders can manage their risk by implementing disciplined strategies, such as using [stop-loss orders](/futures/blogs/stop-loss-orders-which-order-type-to-use/) to automatically close positions at set prices, thereby limiting potential losses.

Navigating the futures markets requires a broad holistic approach. Diversifying your trading across multiple futures complexes like financials, commodities, and currencies can help reduce your exposure to a specific market and balance any losses in one area with potential gains in others.

Trading with smaller position sizes and [Micro contracts](/futures/futures-contracts/micro-emini-futures/) can help mitigate your risk. By limiting the contract size and the number of contracts you trade, you can minimize potential losses. This approach suits beginners and those with limited capital. As your experience grows, you can gradually increase your position sizes while maintaining your risk management plan

### 2. Create your daily market check-in routine

In addition to risk management strategies, successful futures trading during volatile markets requires a good understanding of market dynamics and technical analysis. Traders should closely monitor market news and economic indicators to anticipate potential market movements. ### 3. Sharpen your technical analysis skills

Analyzing past price data to discern trends and patterns enables traders to predict and respond to future price movements more effectively. By combining risk management strategies with a thorough understanding of market dynamics and [technical analysis](/learn/technical-analysis/), traders can increase their chances of success in the volatile futures markets.

Here are some trading strategies and indicators to help you trade during volatile markets.

#### Top 5 futures trading strategies for volatile markets

Each of these strategies has its own advantages and disadvantages.

* **Trend following** involves leveraging existing market trends to capture profits, though it carries the risk of losses in non-trending markets.

* **Range trading**involves identifying support and resistance levels and trading within those ranges. It works well in range-bound markets as markets reverse at key levels, but it falls apart when the market trends.
* **Scalping** involves taking advantage of small price movements over a short period. It can be very intensive and requires a high level of skill and experience.
* **Pairs trading**involves taking advantage of price discrepancies between two related futures contracts. It can be an effective way to reduce the potential of trade risk, but it can be complex for new traders.
* **Hedging** involves using futures contracts to offset risk in other investments. It can be an effective way to reduce risk, but it can also limit potential profits.

The best futures trading strategy for volatile markets depends on the individual trader's risk tolerance and experience level. Trend following and range-bound trading are considered less risky than scalping. However, all these strategies have the potential for profitability in a knowledgeable trader’s hands.

#### Technical indicators for spotting volatile market trends

Technical indicators are calculations used to analyze price movements and identify potential trading opportunities. They can be particularly useful in volatile markets, as they can help you identify trends and reversals as well as overbought and oversold conditions.

Some of the most effective technical indicators for spotting volatile market trends include:

* [**Bollinger Bands**](/futures/blogs/bollinger-bands-explained-a-futures-traders-guide/)**:** These consist of three lines: an upper band, a lower band, and a middle band (moving average). The upper and lower bands are set a specified number of standard deviations above and below the moving average. When prices move outside them, it can indicate overbought or oversold conditions and a potential reversal.
* **Average true range (ATR):**The ATR measures the average high-low range of prices (the difference between the high and low prices) over a specified number of periods. A rising ATR indicates increasing volatility, while a falling ATR indicates decreasing volatility.
* [**Relative strength index (RSI)**](/futures/blogs/relative-strength-index-in-futures-trading/)**:**The RSI measures the momentum of price movements by comparing the magnitude of recent highs to recent lows. An RSI reading above 70 indicates overbought conditions, while a reading below 30 indicates oversold conditions.
* **Stochastic oscillator:**Similar to the RSI, the stochastic oscillator measures the relationship between the closing price and the price range over a specified number of periods. A reading above 80 indicates overbought conditions, while a reading below 20 indicates oversold conditions.

These are just a few of the many technical indicators you can use to spot volatile market trends, gain a better understanding of market conditions, and identify potential trading opportunities.
