# What the FOMC Fed Announcement Could Mean for Futures Traders

By
NinjaTrader Team

September 19, 2024

The FOMC ended the suspense by answering the question, “25 or 50 bps?” with a dovish 50 basis point rate cut, spiking [E-](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-s-p-500/)[m](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-s-p-500/)[ini S](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-s-p-500/)[&P futures](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-s-p-500/) in an all-time high of 5,755.75. This represented a 0.96% gain in under two minutes, before retracing back to 5,700, which is where S&P futures were trading just before the announcement. [E-](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/)[m](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/)[ini Dow](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/)[futu](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/)[r](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/)[es](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-dow/) (42,416) and [Gold](/futures/futures-contracts/metals/gold/) (2,627.2) also had similar moves, making all-time highs in the process.

![FOMC rate cut](/getmedia/c1076d8d-ee14-45a6-a021-863af4b8b658/Fed-blog-1.png)

## What Futures Traders Can Take Away from the September FOMC Fed Announcement

Traders should be cautious when trading in front of a news event such as a Fed announcement, as [volatility](/learn/technical-analysis/use-volatility-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/) can increase dramatically. While that can be enticing from a “catch a trend” aspect, it can also introduce risk that a [stop limit order](/futures/blogs/stop-loss-orders-which-order-type-to-use/) might not contain. Consider this: 96% of the trading range in the E-mini S&P futures occurred in the 2 minutes after the FOMC announcement (though the market did make a slightly lower low near the end of regular trading hours before rallying 15 points into the session close). This velocity in which price moved is a reason why some traders wait for some of the dust to settle before re-entering the market. 

![FOMC rate cut](/getmedia/9e50fce6-9db0-42a5-a74a-0578a50ba2a3/Fed-blog-2.png)   
Despite a 50 basis point rate cut, which only became a possibility when the jobs market showed some signs of weakening, [B](/futures/futures-contracts/us-treasury-bonds/)[ond futures](/futures/futures-contracts/us-treasury-bonds/) (ZB) did not get a sustainable boost with this news, suggesting that the treasury futures market may have had a 50-basis point cut already priced in. Gold also saw a decline after making new highs, falling from the R2 Pivot level (2,625.5) until it found support at S2 (2,572.7) before rallying to S1 (2584.6) to finish the session. 

![FOMC rate cuts](/getmedia/84985464-a6fe-4a5a-b9b6-5b0f9aa51d48/Fed-rate-cuts-3.png)

Overall, this announcement seemed to cool most markets, with the [E-mini Russell 2000](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-russell/) and [Micro-Bitcoin futures](/futures/futures-contracts/cryptocurrency/bitcoin/micro-bitcoin/) finishing positive on the day. Fed speakers will begin communicating their messages on Friday, as market participants will speculate on the Fed's next move in the November meeting.
