By
NinjaTrader Team

With industrial production reports coming in cool and manufacturing numbers weakening on a global basis, the possibility for a recession still exists, though it could happen later than most market participants think. How does this information affect prices in commodities markets? Bob Iaccino joined us this week to discuss recent history and outlooks for CME Group markets.  
  
  
  
**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Risk-on/risk-off assets
* What it means for a double top or double bottom to be “technically correct”
* Effect of El Nino/La Nina weather events on the price of crude oil
* Future of shale oil wells in North America
* Chart analysis of Gold and Crude Oil futures
