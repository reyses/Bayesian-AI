# NinjaTrader Wins Two Benzinga Awards: “Best Brokerage for Trading Futures” for Third Year and Most Transformative M&A

December 13, 2022

![Global Fintech Awards Winner 2022](/NT/media/Documents/Benzinga_NinjaTrader_Best_Brokerage_2022_Banner.png)

We are proud to be recognized at the Benzinga Global Fintech Awards held on December 8, 2022. During the ceremony, NinjaTrader was awarded two [2022 Benzinga Global Fintech Awards](http://www.benzinga.com/fintech/22/12/30015602/exclusive-benzinga-announces-2022-global-fintech-award-winners-see-whos-moving-the-industry-forward): Best Brokerage for Trading Futures and Most Transformative M&A.

## NinjaTrader Recognized as Best Brokerage for Trading Futures

Honored as a “Best Brokerage for Trading Futures” for a third consecutive year, NinjaTrader is proud to continue to uphold our mission to make futures more accessible and meet the growing demand for innovative solutions among the trading community.

Winning this award is confirmation for us to continue to create an informed and confident futures trading community; one that is equally confident in NinjaTrader as their brokerage and trading platform.

## NinjaTrader Wins Award for Most Transformative M&A

Additionally, being recognized with the “Most Transformative M&A” award marks our commitment to disrupt the futures trading industry through thoughtful M&A, including our most recent acquisition of Tradovate LLC. Such recognition allows us to continue to identify and execute strategic expansion efforts and lower the barrier of entry to trading futures globally.

Thank you NinjaTrader community for your support!
