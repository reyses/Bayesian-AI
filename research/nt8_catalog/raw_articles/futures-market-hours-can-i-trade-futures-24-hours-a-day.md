# Futures Trading Hours: Can You Trade Futures 24/7?

By
NinjaTrader

May 15, 2025

Of the many advantages futures trading offers over other asset types, the ability to trade around the clock, outside of traditional market hours, is exceptionally valuable.

## How to Trade Futures After Hours

Futures markets trade nearly 24/6, from Sunday at 6:00 pm ET to Friday at 5:00 pm ET. Compared to stock and ETF traders’ relatively shorter trading sessions of only 6.5/5, futures traders have ample time to trade, giving them more trading flexibility and the freedom to manage positions almost any time of day.

For equity index traders, E-mini and [Micro E-mini futures](/Micro-Emini-Futures) allow traders to participate in the same markets as Wall Street before and after the stock market’s relatively short trading session. Index traders can more effectively take advantage of events such as earnings releases, which occur outside of normal stock market trading hours.

## Futures Trading Hours

Can you Trade Futures on Weekends? Trading hours for futures contracts are determined by their respective exchanges. Each category, such as energy or equity index, has its own opening and closing times.

View the trading hours for each futures exchange here:

* [Chicago Mercantile Exchange](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading-hours.html) (CME)
* [Intercontinental Exchange](https://www.theice.com/productguide/Search.shtml?tradingHours=) (ICE)
* [Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/trade/trading-hours)
* [Chicago Board Options Exchange](http://www.cboe.com/products/contract-specs-and-trading-hours/trading-hours-and-holidays) (CBOE)

## US Futures Market Traded Worldwide

The nearly 24-hour trading access for US futures attracts participants from across the globe. International traders regularly participate in the transparent and regulated futures marketplace with abundant trading opportunities every day. This includes categories like agriculture, crude oil, energy, equity index, interest rates, metals, real estate and weather.

U.S. equity markets are well-established economic barometers of the global economy and have an international following. Traders worldwide speculate the Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Russell indexes daily via E-mini and Micro E-mini futures.

## Benefits of Futures Trading Hours

Above all, the ability to trade almost any time of day provides significant convenience for futures traders. It can help traders find market opportunities, get comfortable with new trading software and analysis tools, and practice in a sim trading environment.

* Determine the best times of day to trade based on your schedule and market approach.
* Find the most appropriate markets to trade with ample time to analyze price and volume action.
* Practice paper trading nearly any time of day with sim trading and real-time market data.
