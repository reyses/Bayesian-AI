### **What Are Trend Lines?**

Trend lines are a foundational tool in technical analysis, used by traders to determine market direction and identify potential support and resistance levels. A trend line is typically drawn by connecting at least two significant price points on a chart, forming either an uptrend, downtrend, or sideways trend, depending on the market's movement.

* **Uptrend:** A series of higher highs and higher lows
* **Downtrend:** A series of lower highs and lower lows
* **Sideways trend (range-bound market):** Price oscillates between defined support and resistance levels without a clear directional bias

Traders use trend lines to validate market trends, recognize breakout patterns, and identify optimal trade entry and exit points.

### **How to Use the TrendLines Indicator**

The TrendLines indicator can be applied to any chart within the NinjaTrader platform. Here's how to get started:

1. First, right-click on the Chart and select **Indicators** or hit Ctrl+I on your keyboard.
2. Double-click on **Trend Lines**in the Available list so it appears in the Configured list at the bottom-left.
3. Configure the Properties on the right, such as the number of trend lines displayed as well as the color, thickness, and style of the lines. The “Strength” field will determine the sensitivity of the automatic trend detection algorithm.
4. Once your parameters are configured, click OK to add it to your Chart.

Once activated, the indicator will display key trend lines in real time, allowing traders to quickly spot support and resistance levels or identify potential trend reversals.

![TrendLines Indicator indicating Swing High and Lower High](/getmedia/9b8148a5-2107-4c01-94f5-0f41f5c64b46/TrendLines-Indicator.png)

On the chart above, the current trend is indicated by an opaque blue line. This is a trend line high, which is based on a swing high followed by a lower high point. The inverse is true for a trend low. Depending on the number of trend lines you specify during the setup of this indicator, previous trend lines will appear, but they are not as prominent scrolling back in time on the chart.

Combining the TrendLines indicator with other tools can provide a more comprehensive market perspective, helping traders validate potential trade setups and improve their overall strategy.
