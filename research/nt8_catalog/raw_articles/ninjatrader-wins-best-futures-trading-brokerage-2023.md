# NinjaTrader Honored with Two Benzinga Awards, Winning “Best Futures Trading Brokerage” for Fourth Year

By
NinjaTrader Team

November 18, 2023

![Benzinga Award 2023](/NT/media/Documents/Blog-Thumbnail-Template-Backup-970-x-250-px.png)

We are honored to be recognized at the Benzinga Global Fintech Awards for the fourth consecutive year. During the ceremony on November 13, 2023, NinjaTrader was recognized with two [2023 Benzinga Global Fintech Awards](https://www.benzinga.com/fintech/23/11/35749196/benzinga-fintech-awards-2023-winners-meet-the-innovators-changing-finance): “Best Brokerage for Trading Futures” and “Developing Best-in-Class Education to Make Futures Accessible to All Traders”.

## NinjaTrader Wins “Best Brokerage for Trading Futures” Award

For the fourth consecutive year, NinjaTrader was honored as “Best Brokerage for Trading Futures”. As traders look to diversify their trading portfolios, we’re further charged with a mission to make futures trading more accessible.    
   
At NinjaTrader, we provide our expanding customer base with cutting-edge products, easy-to-use technology, and a wide variety of services. We are always striving to be at the forefront of innovation and to offer our clients the best possible experience.

## NinjaTrader Wins “Developing Best-in-Class Education to Make Futures Accessible to All Traders” Award

By continuously innovating with new features and providing training resources designed to help reduce the learning curve, we’re giving our users and aspiring futures traders opportunities to grow their skills and transition from other asset classes into futures.    
   
We will continue to expand our futures trading education in diversified formats. This includes hands-on training and timely market commentary from industry professionals to help new futures traders build familiarity with the futures markets.
