# Why Trade Nasdaq Futures vs. FAANG Stocks?

By
NinjaTrader Team

August 20, 2024

![Nasdaq futures vs. FAANG stocks](/NT/media/images/Blogpost-Banner-Image-Template-1.png)

When considering what instruments to trade—[stocks or futures](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/)—traders are often drawn to big stock names like the FAANG (Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google) stocks. These companies are some of the largest in the world but come with numerous challenges for traders, like high stock prices that can make them expensive to trade. Traders looking for a lower capital alternative can explore [Nasdaq 100 futures](/futures/blogs/fundamental-analysis-of-nasdaq-100-futures/), which offer several distinct advantages over trading individual FAANG stocks. Read more to learn more about these advantages.

## 1. Diversification and Reduced Risk

[E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures](/futures/futures-contracts/equity-index/e-mini-nasdaq/) represent a basket of the 100 largest non-financial companies listed on the Nasdaq exchange. This improved diversification reduces the risk compared to trading individual FAANG stocks with their earnings and economic risks. If one company underperforms, the impact on the overall index is mitigated by the performance of other companies in the index. This diversification helps traders reduce and manage their risk more effectively.

## 2. Leverage and Capital Efficiency

Futures contracts allow traders to control larger positions with less capital. This leverage can magnify gains, making it possible for significant returns from smaller market movements. But this added leverage also comes with the potential for greater losses. For example, with a day trading margin requirement of just a few hundred dollars, a trader can control a Micro Nasdaq 100 futures contract. This capital efficiency is a key reason traders choose futures over individual FAANG stocks.

## 3. Tax Advantages

[Trading futures](/futures/why-trade-futures/futures-vs-stocks/) contracts can offer favorable tax treatment compared to trading individual stocks. In the U.S., futures traders can benefit from the 60/40 rule, where 60% of profits are taxed as long-term capital gains and 40% as short-term gains, regardless of the holding period. This tax advantage can result in significant savings for active profitable traders.

## 4. Trading Hours and Liquidity

E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures trade nearly 24 hours a day, providing more flexibility for traders to react to global events and news. In contrast, FAANG stocks trade primarily during regular market hours. The extended trading hours of futures allow for better [risk management](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) and the ability to enter or exit positions outside of standard trading times. Additionally, E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures generally offer deep liquidity, ensuring traders can execute large orders without significant price impact.

## 5. Cost Efficiency

Trading futures can offer lower transaction costs compared to trading individual stocks. Futures traders typically pay lower commissions, and there are no borrowing costs for short positions. This cost efficiency can help enhance overall trading profitability, especially for high-frequency traders.

While trading FAANG stocks can be fun and exciting, E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures offer several more practical trading advantages—including diversification, leverage, tax benefits, extended trading hours, and cost efficiency. These benefits make E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures a more attractive option for traders looking to capitalize on market movements with greater flexibility and the ability to better manage risk.
