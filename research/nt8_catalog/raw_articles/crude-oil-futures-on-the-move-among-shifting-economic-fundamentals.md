# Crude Oil Futures on the Move Among Shifting Economic Fundamentals

By
NinjaTrader Team

August 10, 2023

The energy markets are on the move as OPEC+ continues to adjust production quotas in light of a global economic slowdown and shifting international allegiances.

Special guest Bob Iaccino joined us this week on “See the Futures” to discuss global and domestic economic fundamentals of the crude oil global marketplace and possible ramifications to the CME Group’s WTI Light Sweet [Crude Oil futures](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/micro-crude-oil-futures/) market.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Crude oil’s impact on CPI numbers
* Rally in heating oil prices
* Refinery capacity
* Traditional vs. shale rigs
* Chart analysis of Crude Oil, Heating Oil and RBOB Gasoline futures
