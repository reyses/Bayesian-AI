# Central Banks in Focus with Erik Norland of CME Group

By
NinjaTrader

June 01, 2023

Monetary policies for the major global central banks are top-of-mind as inflation and the threat of recession dominate traders' concerns. Special guest Erik Norland, executive director, senior economist, CME Group, joins us to discuss the give and take between the Federal Reserve Bank, European Central Bank and the Bank of England.

## Erik Norland on CME Group futures markets, currency futures

Various interest rate and quantitative easing/tightening policies affect the flow of international funds. CME Group futures markets, including currency futures, are on the move.

**Additional topics covered in the livestream include:**

* An explanation of inflation in the United Kingdom
* A breakdown of four key components that comprise currency prices
* Analysis of the differences in commercial vs. real estate sectors
* A discussion on why China’s COVID recovery has fallen short of expectations
* An opinion on the yield curve as a tool for economic forecasting
* A look into the unique challenges for the ECB and member countries
