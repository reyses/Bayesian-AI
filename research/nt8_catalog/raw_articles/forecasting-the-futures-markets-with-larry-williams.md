# Forecasting the Futures Markets With Larry Williams

By
NinjaTrader Team

March 21, 2024

As a futures trader, you’re tasked with constantly navigating the dynamic landscape of the futures markets, which requires having a broad understanding of market-related concepts and analysis to help you make more informed trading decisions.

Futures traders must identify and adapt to ever-changing market conditions using a variety of technical analysis indicators in price charts. Traders of all levels can benefit from watching experienced traders talk through their chart analysis to help them expand their knowledge and understanding of the markets.

To that end, Larry Williams—renowned trader, author, and innovator in the world of futures trading—joined our broadcast to provide a comprehensive year-to-date review and forward analysis of several key futures markets.

Throughout his decades-long career, Larry has had a profound impact on futures trading strategies and market analysis. He’s helped thousands of traders improve their trading by his example, trading with real dollars and notably winning the Robbins World Cup Championship of Futures Trading in 1987 by turning $10,000 into over $1.1 million within one year.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Key futures markets Larry is tracking right now
* What is the [Commitment of Traders (COT)](/futures/blogs/what-is-the-cftc-commitment-of-traders-report/) report?
* How to incorporate cyclical analysis in your trading
* Tracking major market index correlations
