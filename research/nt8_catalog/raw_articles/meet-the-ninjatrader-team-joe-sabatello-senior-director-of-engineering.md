# Meet the NinjaTrader Team: Joe Sabatello, Senior Director of Engineering

By
NinjaTrader Team

August 07, 2024

In a recent episode of NinjaTrader Live, we sat down with Joe Sabatello, senior director of engineering at NinjaTrader, to discuss his career journey and various impactful projects and initiatives he’s worked on at NinjaTrader. Watch to learn about his role and what NinjaTrader’s technological innovations may mean for traders like you.

**Topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* High-frequency trading
* [Futures trading](/futures/why-trade-futures/) innovation
* NinjaTrader’s vision for the future

## A Journey Through Trading Technology

Beginning his career at WH Trading, Joe immersed himself in the fast-paced world of proprietary and high-frequency trading (HFT). This experience laid a strong foundation for his understanding of trading technologies and market dynamics. He later transitioned to Transact, where he shifted his focus to enhancing the retail trading experience.

### Understanding High-Frequency Trading

Joe shared some of the intricacies of HFT, explaining how it involves processing transactions within microseconds, how data is mission critical, and how HFT is increasing utilizing automation and AI. This level of speed and precision is essential for large proprietary traders, but this technology also benefits futures traders of all levels.

### Joining NinjaTrader

Joe talked about his experience and insights during the integration of Transact Futures with NinjaTrader following the acquisition. The challenge was to scale the system to handle a larger number of accounts while ensuring a seamless user experience. Joe talked about how he enjoyed working on that project, reconciling trades and data in real time and enhancing backend services to allow for more growth and expansion.

### Embracing Technological Advancements

Things are moving fast, and Joe emphasized the importance of staying updated with technological advancements—particularly the trend from physical data centers to cloud-based solutions. This transition offers greater flexibility and scalability, allowing NinjaTrader to adapt to changing needs quickly and handle large volumes of data more efficiently.

### Prospects and Personal Insights

Looking ahead, Joe hints at exciting developments at NinjaTrader, focused on enhancing the user experience, staying ahead of the technology curve, and maintaining NinjaTrader’s position as the best [trading platform](/trading-platform/) for futures traders of all levels.

On a personal note, Joe shares his experiences of balancing work with family life and his passion for the Purdue Boilermakers.

### Continuing to Drive Innovation

Joe Sabatello's journey and tenure at NinjaTrader are a testament to his dedication and expertise in the trading technology industry. As a key member of the NinjaTrader team, his contributions continue to drive innovation and excellence in our trading community. Stay tuned for more exciting updates from Joe and the NinjaTrader team.
