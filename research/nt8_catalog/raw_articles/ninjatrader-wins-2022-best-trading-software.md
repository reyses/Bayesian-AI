# NinjaTrader Wins 2022 Best Trading Software

By
NinjaTrader

February 21, 2022

NinjaTrader is proud to announce its selection as a Best Trading Software in the Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities (TASC) magazine 2022 Readers’ Choice Awards.

NinjaTrader was voted Best Trading Software in the “< $500” and “> $1000” pricing categories and recognized in six other categories including:

* Software Between $500 – $1000
* Institutional Platform
* Professional Trading Platform
* Institutional Platform
* Futures Brokerage
* Forex Brokerage

For fourteen consecutive years, the readers of TASC have recognized NinjaTrader as a premier destination for both new and experienced traders. This award gives readers the chance to decide which products and services outperform the competition and deserve to be recognized. The best and most innovative trading solutions are awarded each year based on votes from traders and market enthusiasts.
