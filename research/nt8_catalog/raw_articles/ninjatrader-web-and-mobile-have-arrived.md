# NinjaTrader Web and Mobile Have Arrived!

By
NinjaTrader

March 04, 2023

![](/NT/media/images/News/NT-Web-and-Mobile-Blog-Header.png)

We are excited to announce the next step in our evolution with the introduction of our cloud-based trading infrastructure & integrated multi-device trading through NinjaTrader Web and Mobile.  
  
As we have continued to enhance our services to best support you, we have also launched a new look and feel across our website and communication channels to reflect our expanded product offerings and leadership role in the futures industry. We hope you like the new look!  
  
Access to NinjaTrader Web & Mobile along with an expanded account dashboard is **now included with your NinjaTrader Brokerage account.**  
  
Manage your account, check prices, place orders, and view charts from anywhere on your mobile device, PC or Mac!  
  
**NinjaTrader Web**

Log in to NinjaTrader Web from any PC or Mac web browser for a fully integrated trading experience. To launch, [log in](https://account.ninjatrader.com/welcome) to your NinjaTrader client dashboard & select “Trade”.

**Mobile Apps**

Take your futures trading with you through an experience optimized for mobile devices and tablets. Download for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ninjatrader.voyager&hl=en_US) or [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ninjatrader-2025-futures/id6473653053).

**Updated Desktop Platform**

A new version of NinjaTrader’s award-winning platform is now available which unlocks further integration across a cloud-based trading infrastructure. [Log in](https://account.ninjatrader.com/welcome) to update to the most recent version of NinjaTrader (v. 8.1).

**Enhanced Account Management Experience**

In addition to the expanded platform options, new account management tools and on-demand training resources are also now available to help support your trading. [Log in](https://account.ninjatrader.com/welcome) to your account dashboard to check out the new experience.  
  
Please visit our [Help Center](https://ninjatrader.com/support) for articles introducing the new tools and functionality along with a detailed [FAQ](https://support.ninjatrader.com/s/article/New-NinjaTrader-Experience-FAQ?language=en_US). If you have additional questions, please contact us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f0838580809f8284b09e999e9a91848291949582de939f9d).  
  
Be sure to follow us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/NinjaTrader) and like us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/NinjaTraderGroup/) to stay connected with all updates for the NinjaTrader community.
