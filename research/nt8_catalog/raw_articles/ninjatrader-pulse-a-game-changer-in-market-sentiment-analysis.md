# Introducing NinjaTrader Pulse: A Game Changer in Market Sentiment Analysis

By
NinjaTrader Team

January 30, 2025

Have you heard of [NinjaTrader](/trading-platform/ninjatrader-pulse/)[Pulse](/trading-platform/ninjatrader-pulse/)? This powerful new sentiment indicator is designed to deliver a real-time pulse on the futures markets by harnessing true market sentiment based on actual traded volume. Pulse offers traders of all experience levels a new way to gain visibility into the collective momentum of the trading community.

## What Does NinjaTrader Pulse Do?

Pulse provides traders with an aggregate view of market sentiment that refreshes every second. Unlike other tools that might rely on forecasts or hypothetical data, Pulse draws from actual trades executed by actual NinjaTrader users. This means you’re seeing the sentiment built on real (live) transactions—giving you the most accurate, current snapshot of market behavior.

## How Does Pulse Work?

Whether you're tracking market momentum or positioning trends, Pulse’s tools provide unparalleled insight into the forces driving price movements. Let’s take a closer look at two essential elements that make Pulse a must-have for serious traders: pressure gauges and true market insight.

#### Pressure Gauges

The heart of Pulse is its intuitive pressure gauges, which show the near real-time market momentum. Every second, these gauges update to reflect the latest market positioning, giving you insights into average long and short prices on key instruments.

Not only can you view these gauges within the platform, but you can arrange them in the module in the view of your choice, making it easier than ever to integrate market sentiment into your trading strategy.

#### True Market Insight

Pulse covers the top 10 instruments (CL, MCL, ES, MES, GC, MGC, NQ, MNQ, YM, and MYM), capturing the majority of market volume. Each gauge offers insights that are product-based, keeping them stable and easy to interpret without the noise of expiration data.

## A New Edge for Every Type of Trader

For those new to futures trading, Pulse serves as a powerful tool to help learn, test, and refine strategies. By observing market sentiment in real time, new traders can understand market dynamics faster and more intuitively. Professional traders, on the other hand, will find Pulse to be an indispensable addition to their trading toolkit, providing a quick, easy-to-access overview that can help refine decision-making on the fly.
