# How to use NinjaTrader’s SuperDOM on mobile

By
NinjaTrader Team

January 23, 2026

Ready to trade futures from your phone? With the NinjaTrader mobile app, you’ve got access to powerful tools designed for active traders, including the SuperDOM, one of the most intuitive ways to view price action and place trades fast.

Whether you’re stepping away from your desktop or trading entirely on mobile, the NinjaTrader mobile SuperDOM makes it easy to manage orders and monitor market depth, all in real time.

## What is the SuperDOM?

If you're new to the SuperDOM, here’s a quick breakdown. **SuperDOM** stands for *Super Depth of Market*. It’s a ladder-style interface that shows you real-time bid and ask prices stacked by level, so you can see exactly how much interest exists at each price point.

Think of it as your control panel for:

* Viewing order flow as it happens.
* Placing and modifying trades directly from the price ladder.
* Tracking your position, entry, and real-time P&L.

The SuperDOM is a go-to tool for many futures traders, and you can use it right from the NinjaTrader app.

[Read our blog on Intro to the NinjaTrader Desktop SuperDOM.](/futures/blogs/intro-to-the-ninjatrader-desktop-superdom/)

## How to access the SuperDOM on the NinjaTrader mobile app

To use the SuperDOM, you’ll need to download the NinjaTrader app on your iOS or Android device and log in to your account.

Here’s how to open the SuperDOM in just a few taps:

1. Open the NinjaTrader app.
2. From the **Watchlist**or **Search**, select the futures contract you want to trade.
3. Tap the **SuperDOM** tab on the instrument’s detail screen.

That’s it; you’re in! Now you’ll see the live price ladder, ready for action.

## How to read the SuperDOM on mobile

The mobile SuperDOM is compact, but it gives you a clear view of what’s happening in the market:

* **Prices** are stacked vertically in the center of the screen.
* **Buy orders (bids)** are listed on the left, showing how many contracts are queued at each level.
* **Sell orders (asks)** are on the right.
* **Your orders and position** appear as color-coded markers directly on the ladder.

You can scroll to see additional price levels or zoom in for more detail. It’s built for fast decisions and clear information—exactly what you need when trading on the go.

[Learn more in our SuperDOM Overview.](/support/helpguides/nt8/NT%20HelpGuide%20English.html?superdom.htm)

## Placing orders using the mobile SuperDOM

Once you're comfortable with the layout, placing trades is simple:

### To enter an order:

1. Tap a price level on the ladder.
2. A trade ticket will open where you can:
   1. Choose your **order type** (Limit, Stop Market, etc.).
   2. Adjust **quantity**.
   3. Set **Time in Force**.
3. Tap Submit to send the order.

### To place a market order:

* Use the **Buy Mkt**or **Sell Mkt** buttons at the bottom of the screen for immediate execution at the best available price.

### To manage an order:

* Tap on your working order in the ladder to **modify** or **cancel** it. You’ll see the option to update price or size or just remove it altogether.

Everything updates in real time, and changes you make on mobile will show up instantly on your web and desktop platforms.

[Read our blog on Placing Limit Orders With the SuperDOM.](/futures/blogs/ninjatrader-mobile-app-placing-limit-orders-with-the-superdom/)

## Master the SuperDOM wherever you trade

The NinjaTrader mobile SuperDOM gives you full control over your trades, no matter where you are. From fast order entry to real-time depth of market, it’s designed to support confident trading on the move.

Whether you're testing strategies in sim, managing a live position, or watching the market develop during your commute, the SuperDOM helps you stay connected and in command.
