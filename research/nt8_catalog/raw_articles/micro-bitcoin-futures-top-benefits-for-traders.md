# Micro Bitcoin Futures: Top Benefits for Traders

By
NinjaTrader Team

February 20, 2025

There’s no denying the impact Bitcoin has made on the trading landscape in recent years. If you’re interested in cryptocurrency trading but hesitant about holding digital currency in a virtual wallet or an exchange, [Micro Bitcoin futures](/futures/futures-contracts/cryptocurrency/bitcoin/micro-bitcoin/) (MBT) provide a compelling alternative. If you’re already invested in cryptocurrencies and looking for an efficient hedge against your long-term holdings, MBT offer a streamlined way to manage risk. These futures bring Bitcoin trading to the masses with enhanced flexibility, accessibility, and efficiency.

## Shorting Bitcoin

One of the significant advantages of MBT is the ability to short Bitcoin without incurring borrowing fees. Unlike stocks or ETFs, where shorting often involves borrowing shares and paying interest, MBT contracts allow traders to sell a contract at their chosen month and post the required margin—simplifying the short-selling process. This makes MBT an attractive choice for traders looking to capitalize on downward price movements.

## **Going Long Without a Spot Account**

If you’re bullish on Bitcoin but don’t want to open a spot account at a crypto exchange, Micro Bitcoin futures provide a practical solution. Offered by CME Group, MBT contracts are sized at 1/10 of a Bitcoin, enabling traders to gain exposure to Bitcoin without directly purchasing it. Additionally, trading MBT requires only about 38% of the notional value, making it a capital-efficient way to participate in Bitcoin’s price action. For example, a futures trader can gain exposure to $3,500 worth of Bitcoin with just $1,330, providing a lower-cost alternative to spot trading.

## **Additional Benefits of Trading Micro Bitcoin Futures**

Beyond the core advantages of shorting and long exposure without a spot account, MBT contracts offer a range of benefits that make them a compelling choice for traders:

* **Regulated and time-tested exchange:** MBT contracts are listed on CME Group, the world’s leading derivatives marketplace. With over 20 years of experience in the futures market, CME Group ensures a secure and regulated trading environment.
* **Centralized pool of liquidity and price:** Unlike fragmented spot markets, MBT contracts operate on a centralized exchange, offering uniform pricing and deeper liquidity. All Bitcoin futures traders experience the same price, settle with the CME CF Bitcoin Reference Rate (BRR), and benefit from a transparent first-in, first-out execution model.
* **Diversified trading portfolio:** Trading MBT through platforms like NinjaTrader allows traders access to a broad range of futures products, including major index futures (e.g., E-Mini S&P 500) and commodity markets (e.g., crude oil, gold). This integration makes it easier for traders to diversify their portfolios within a single account.

## **Explore the Unique Benefits of Trading Micro Bitcoin Futures**

Micro Bitcoin futures offer a regulated, capital-efficient, and flexible way to trade Bitcoin. Whether you’re looking to gain exposure, hedge existing holdings, or short Bitcoin without borrowing costs, MBT contracts provide a robust solution.
