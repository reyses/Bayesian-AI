# Meet the NinjaTrader Team: Wade Headrick, Senior Director of Business Development

By
NinjaTrader Team

January 16, 2025

We talked with NinjaTrader Senior Director of Business Development Wade Headrick about his extensive career at NinjaTrader and how he’s been an integral part of the company’s growth, evolution, and development of groundbreaking services and initiatives for the past decade.

## Wade’s NinjaTrader Journey

Wade began his NinjaTrader career in 2015, marking nearly 10 years of contributions to the company's growth. Starting as an inside sales associate, he moved up through the ranks, becoming sales manager and director of sales before taking on his current role leading the business development team.

Wade’s journey within the company has mirrored NinjaTrader’s evolution from a platform-focused software company to an industry-leading futures brokerage firm.

“Having witnessed the company grow over the years has been incredible," said Wade. "We've really transformed how we support traders by integrating our technology and brokerage services under one roof.”

## The Evolution of NinjaTrader’s Business Model

A key focus of our conversation with Wade was the evolution of NinjaTrader’s business philosophy: how the integration of brokerage services, data, and advanced technology has streamlined the trader experience, providing a single comprehensive source of futures trading services.

“We now have the ability to offer everything a futures trader needs, all in one place—from platform tools to market data to clearing services—making it easier than ever for traders to get the support they need," said Wade.

## The Role of the NinjaTrader Ecosystem

One of the features of NinjaTrader is the NinjaTrader Ecosystem, which connects third-party add-on vendors with NinjaTrader clients. The Ecosystem helps support customer success with the ability to import advanced analytical tools, create custom indicators and strategies, and provide education and mentoring services.

"The Ecosystem is designed to offer more than just software. We’re focused on providing tools that help traders succeed in real time—including risk management, trading psychology, and many other trader support resources,” said Wade. “And with the Ecosystem’s expansion, currently underway, we will continue to provide an ever-increasing array of services tailored to traders of all types and levels.”

## New Initiatives and Tools for Traders

One of the most exciting developments Wade highlighted was the new developer site, which consolidates all the documentation and support resources for NinjaTrader’s technologies. This centralized hub makes it easier for developers and traders alike to get the information they need to make the most out of NinjaTrader’s tools.

## The Impact of AI and Trader Support

AI is another hot topic in the trading world, and Wade shared his thoughts on how this technology is shaping the future of trading. While AI presents opportunities for developing more advanced strategies and indicators, it also brings challenges in terms of guiding traders through complex market conditions. With the integration of AI with NinjaTrader’s client services team, we can now offer enhanced support for traders who utilize new AI tools.

## The Rise of Retail Trading and Market Accessibility

Wade reflected on the significant increase in retail trading, particularly since 2020, as more people embraced online trading during the pandemic. NinjaTrader has been helping to democratize futures trading all along, making futures trading more accessible to a broader audience, including younger traders, retirees, and those looking for a second income stream.

“Tools like [M](/futures/futures-contracts/micro-emini-futures/)[icro contracts](/futures/futures-contracts/micro-emini-futures/) have made it easier than ever for more people to participate in the futures markets," said Wade. “But with all things trading, it’s important to have a consistent [risk management plan](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) in place and practice in a simulator before risking real dollars.”

As the conversation came to a close, Wade shared his excitement about the future of NinjaTrader. He highlighted ongoing projects that will continue to enhance the NinjaTrader experience by providing unique tools, services, and resources to help traders stay ahead of the crowd.

"We’re always thinking about the next steps in improving the platform and providing valuable educational resources to our traders," said Wade. "Community and education are at the heart of what we do at NinjaTrader."
