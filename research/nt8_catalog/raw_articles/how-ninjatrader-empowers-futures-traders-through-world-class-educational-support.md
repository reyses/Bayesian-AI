## Futures Trading 101 Series: Develop the Trader in You and Technical Analysis Made Easy

For those new to futures trading, NinjaTrader offers a complimentary Futures Trading 101 series, designed to help traders build a solid foundation in futures. This series consists of two main courses:

* [**Develop the Trader In You:**](/learn/develop-the-trader-in-you/) This course is tailored for beginners, introducing the basics of futures trading and guiding traders through key concepts such as leverage, order types, and the role of margin. By the end of this course, traders gain a strong understanding of how futures markets work and what it takes to trade effectively.
* [**Technical Analysis Made Easy:**](/learn/technical-analysis-made-easy/) Once traders have mastered the basics, this course teaches the essentials of technical analysis, including chart patterns, trendlines, and common indicators. By focusing on practical, easy-to-understand concepts, this series empowers traders to make informed decisions based on technical signals.

These courses provide a structured path for developing a future trader's knowledge and confidence, making the transition from learning to trading smoother.
