## How to Build Your Futures Trading Plan and Routine

A well-structured trading routine can help you set the tone for the day by identifying potential trading opportunities. It may include:

* Premarket analysis, where traders review overnight market movements
* Economic news
* Upcoming news events that could impact market prices

A consistent approach to technical analysis can be another vital element in your daily trading plan and routine. The more you view historical and real-time charts across a variety of symbols and time frames, the better you become at identifying potential trade setups and situations to avoid.

Position and risk management are also fundamental to maximizing gains and managing risk. This involves setting stop-loss orders, adjusting position size, and regularly reviewing your trading history to reinforce positive skills and reduce the impact of negative actions.
