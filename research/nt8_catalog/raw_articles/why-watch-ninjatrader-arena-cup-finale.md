## FAQs about the NinjaTrader Arena Cup Finale

What is the NinjaTrader Arena Cup?

The NinjaTrader Arena Cup is NinjaTrader's biggest simulated futures trading competition, with $250,000 in total cash prizes across all rounds. Traders competed on live CME market data using the [NinjaTrader Arena](/ninjatrader-arena/), with no real capital at stake. The top 30 performers from each qualifying round advanced to the invite-only finale, plus 10 wild card spots, for a total of 100 finalists.

What was the prize pool for the Arena Cup Finale?

Finalists competed on June 9 for $190,000 in prizes, including a $100,000 grand prize for the top finisher and $90,000 distributed across the other top performers. The full Arena Cup competition—including all three qualifying rounds—carried a $250,000 total prize pool.

What does the replay include?

The NinjaTrader Live replay of the Arena Cup Finale includes real-time leaderboard updates, live trade breakdowns, expert commentary from professional traders and influencers, and the $100,000 grand prize winner announcement at the close of trading.

What futures contracts are traded in Arena competitions?

Qualifying rounds featured a defined set of Micro contracts: Micro E-mini S&P 500 (MES), Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ), Micro gold (MGC), Micro crude oil (MCL), and Micro Euro FX (M6E). The championship final had its own contract list, which finalists received ahead of the event.
