# Micro Bitcoin Futures: Cryptocurrencies Surging in Price, Trader Interest

By
NinjaTrader Team

March 28, 2024

The recent resurgence of cryptocurrency, sparked by new all-time high prices, marks a significant turnaround from the market's previous lows, capturing the attention of investors, traders, and technologists.

This revival can be attributed to several key factors, including increased adoption by mainstream financial markets in the form of ETFs. There have also been advancements in blockchain technology and a growing interest in digital assets as a hedge against traditional financial market volatility.

As the regulatory landscape continues to evolve, offering clearer guidelines and protections for investors, the cryptocurrency market is poised for continued growth, which may mark the beginning of a new era in digital finance.

[Micro Bitcoin futures](/futures/futures-contracts/cryptocurrency/bitcoin/micro-bitcoin/) present an opportunity for traders who want to engage with the world of digital currencies without substantial capital associated with traditional Bitcoin investments. This lower-investment approach allows traders to diversify their trading with smaller fractions of Bitcoin, making it more accessible to a broader audience and offering a unique way to mitigate risk in a highly volatile market.  

In this segment of Develop Your Edge, Anthony Crudele discusses why cryptocurrencies are enjoying a resurgence in price and trader interest and how you can tap into this exciting market with Micro Bitcoin futures contracts.
