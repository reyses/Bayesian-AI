## How are E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures Used by Traders Around the World?

The E-mini Nasdaq 100 is a vital global trading instrument representing both domestic and international companies. By trading Nasdaq 100 futures, international traders can gain direct, efficient exposure to the technology sector’s performance without needing to invest directly in a large portfolio of stocks, which can come with foreign currency exchange risk.

### What Can Affect the Price of E-mini Nasdaq 100 Index futures?

Like most financial futures, economic indicators are important fundamental factors that can influence the price of E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures. Likewise, domestic and global economic health, geopolitical events, and the strength of the U.S. dollar (USD) can all impact trader sentiment. As these factors are often interconnected, it's important to track economic reports and news in combination with your trading.

**Economic Indicators**

The daily flow of economic data often has a direct and immediate impact on market sentiment and can cause significant price fluctuations in the futures markets as traders position themselves for their short-term, midterm, and long-term investment outlooks. Key reports to follow include gross domestic product (GDP), inflation, the U.S. employment situation, and others.

**USD**

The USD plays a significant and sometimes complex role in the pricing of E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures. A strong USD can negatively affect the earnings of companies with substantial international operations; when the dollar is strong, revenues earned in foreign currencies are worth fewer dollars when converted back. Also, many commodities are priced in USD, making them more expensive, which can increase costs and reduce profits.

**Geopolitical And Geoeconomic Factors**

Negative geopolitical and geoeconomic factors can increase risk and affect investor sentiment, which can often drive the price of E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures lower. Things to look out for include energy supply disruptions, political instability, changes in trade policies, and international conflicts.

### How to Track Earnings Reports Using Nasdaq-listed Companies

Tracking the earnings reports of large Nasdaq-listed companies is crucial for traders as these reports can significantly influence market sentiment and short-term price action. Earnings reports provide key insights into a company's financial health, profitability, and future outlook, which can directly impact the stock price and the broader index if it's a large component of that index.

For instance, if major companies like Apple or Amazon report strong earnings, it can boost investor confidence and drive market-wide rallies. Conversely, weaker than expected earnings from these giants can trigger selloffs. For active traders, staying aware of when earnings are released allows them to adjust their trading and positions proactively. ### Important E-mini NQ 100 Intermarket Analysis Considerations

Intermarket relationships refer to the connections and correlations between different futures markets, and understanding these relationships can provide valuable insights for traders when formulating a directional bias for a specific market. For Nasdaq 100 E-mini futures, there are several key intermarket relationships to watch.

**Bonds and Interest Rates****:**Typically, when bond prices rise (and interest rates fall), it can be positive for the stock market as lower interest rate yields make stock buying more appealing to investors. In addition, lower interest rates (borrowing costs) can help companies expand and boost corporate profits. Conversely, rising interest rates (falling bond prices) can make fixed income investments more attractive, creating less demand for stocks; and higher borrowing costs might depress corporate earnings due to lower consumer spending. The relationship between the 10-year Treasury yield and the Nasdaq 100 is something traders should always watch.

**USD Index:**The impact of a stronger USD on the Nasdaq 100, particularly due to the tech-heavy nature of the index, can be more negative than positive. Large technology companies often have significant international exposure, generating a large portion of their revenues outside the U.S. in a less competitive environment. In addition, when the USD strengthens, overseas income is worth less when converted back to USD, which can also hurt their earnings and, consequently, their stock prices.

### Seasonality Patterns of E-mini Nasdaq 100 Index futures

Seasonality is the analysis of historical price patterns throughout the year. The current E-mini Nasdaq 100 futures seasonality 15-year pattern (black line) has in recent years seen prices rise from the end of January through the end of April, then sideways prices through the end of June, a rally in July, lower prices in mid-August, then higher prices through the remainder of the year with a few minor pullbacks. (Figure 1)

It is important to note that seasonality is a general guideline for possible trends and not a measure of magnitude. Trends can vary significantly from year to year.

### Using Technical Analysis to Trade on the E-mini Nasdaq 100

In addition to fundamental analysis, most traders also utilize [technical analysis](/learn/technical-analysis/%20) to help determine entry and exit points. Technical indicators can be used on price charts to help analyze price trends and identify chart patterns and key trading levels.

There are several futures-centric indicators popular with traders, including Floor Trader Pivots, Commodity Channel Index (CCI), [Ichimoku Cloud](/futures/blogs/using-the-ichimoku-cloud-in-futures-trading/%20), [VWAP](/futures/blogs/what-is-volume-weighted-average-price-vwap/%20), and NinjaTrader Order Flow Volume Profile.
