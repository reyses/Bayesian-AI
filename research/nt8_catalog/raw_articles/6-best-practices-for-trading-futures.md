# 6 Best Practices for Trading Futures

By
NinjaTrader Team

August 19, 2025

New traders often enter the markets with grand expectations but little preparation, making them especially vulnerable to common pitfalls like emotional decision-making and overtrading, all with little or no risk management. Without a strong market foundation or effective mentoring, many new traders are quickly overwhelmed and then discouraged after their first losing streak. This critical early phase can make or break a trading journey.

That’s why structured, accessible education and real-time resources are essential not just when you’re starting out but as you evolve as a trader—especially during those crucial moments when things are going south and your confidence starts breaking down, which can lead to costly mistakes. Here are six best practices for trading futures.

## 1. Know what you’re trading

Futures markets each have their own rules, nuances, and key dates that can directly impact your trades. Understanding the specifics of the contracts you trade can help you avoid costly surprises.

* Review contract specifications for the markets you’re trading.
* Track [expiration dates](/futures/blogs/understanding-futures-expiration-and-first-notice-date/), last trading day, and rollover schedules.
* Understand the first notice date (FND) for deliverable contracts.
* Learn how point values and tick sizes translate into P&L.
* Monitor market hours and be aware of holiday trading schedules.
* Know your capital requirements and which [margin types](/futures/blogs/understanding-margin-in-futures-trading/) apply to your approach.

Knowing your contracts inside and out can help you make informed, timely trading decisions in any market condition.

## 2. Master order execution

How you place orders can be just as important as the strategy behind them. Having a solid understanding of order types and execution tools can help you act decisively and avoid slippage or missed opportunities. Work towards mastering:

* The differences between market, limit, and stop orders
* Advanced [order types](/futures/blogs/three-basic-order-types-explained/) like stop-limit and market-if-touched (MIT)
* Trade management strategies such as OCO (one cancels other), trailing stops, and break-even stops
* Placing and managing orders directly from charts
* Using the [SuperDOM](/futures/blogs/intro-to-the-ninjatrader-desktop-superdom/) to monitor market depth and quickly execute orders
* Tracking positions and account balances in the control panel
* Practicing in a [simulation environment](/trading-platform/trading-simulator/) before going live

With consistent practice, mastering order execution can help you trade more efficiently and reduce the stress of fast-moving markets.

## 3. Know your risk

Every trade carries risk, and knowing the scale of that risk before you enter is essential. This means understanding how contract size, leverage, and volatility can affect your account balance.

* Begin with micro contracts for more precise position sizing.
* Understand how leverage and margin can amplify both gains and losses.
* Calculate how price moves can affect your P&L.
* Monitor key economic and news events.
* Track market [volatility trends](/learn/technical-analysis/use-volatility-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/).
* Have a backup plan for technology outages.

When you fully understand your exposure, you’ll be better prepared to protect your capital and trade with confidence.

## 4. Manage your risk

Successful traders don’t just manage trades—they manage the risks that come with them. This includes protecting your account from significant drawdowns through consistent position sizing and disciplined stop placement.

* Only trade with risk capital you can afford to lose.
* Set stop-loss and profit target orders with each trade.
* Determine proper position sizing based on your capital and risk tolerance.
* Monitor your open positions and adjust them as conditions change.

Effective [risk management](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) can help you stay in the market longer and weather the inevitable losing streaks that come with trading.

## 5. Put up risk guardrails

Trading discipline isn’t just about what you do during a trade—it’s also about the protections you set up before you even enter the market. Sometimes, the best way to manage risk is to set hard limits before you trade. Risk guardrails can automatically stop trading activity when your preset loss or profit thresholds are hit, including:

* Daily and weekly loss limits
* Daily and weekly profit triggers
* End-of-day trailing max drawdown
* Real-time trailing max drawdown

By setting clear guardrails, you can help prevent emotional decisions and stick to your trading plan even when the markets get volatile.

## 6. Expand your education and growth

The markets never stop evolving, and neither should you as a trader. Staying engaged with new strategies, community discussions, and performance reviews can help you continuously improve on your trading journey.

* Watch [NinjaTrader Live](/learn/livestreams/) every trading day for real-time market analysis and trade discussion from the pros.
* Find a mentor and/or join a trading community.
* Track and review your performance regularly.
* Develop and refine your trading plan.

By committing to ongoing education, you can adapt to changing markets and maintain a competitive edge in your trading.

## Put best practices in action and see the difference

Following these best practices for trading futures can provide powerful benefits. Just knowing and practicing these ideas can help you gain clarity, structure, and the confidence to navigate the markets with more discipline, awareness, and skill.

Reviewing these concepts often— especially when you find yourself struggling—can help reinforce good trading behavior and get you back on track.
