## What is seasonality?

Seasonality is a broad umbrella term for cyclical patterns that can be explained by underlying fundamental or economic factors like supply and demand, weather patterns, and others. The impact of celestial factors like sunspots and moon phases has been studied for decades and can create a different set of seasonal cycles.

* How light and energy levels can alter trader performance
* How to integrate non-traditional cycles into your trading

### Non-Conventional Seasonality

The idea that gravitational forces or levels of light and energy from celestial bodies can affect human emotions and market psychology may seem more mythical than analytical. However, traders and market analysts have been studying these phenomena for many years, and the fact that we're still talking about them today reflects a continued interest in exploring these alternative ideas and concepts.

Sunspots, which are temporary phenomena on the sun's surface, follow an 11-year cycle. Some traders hypothesize that sunspot cycles could correlate with economic booms and busts, as increased solar activity might impact human behavior and market sentiment.

Full moons are sometimes associated with heightened [volatility](/learn/technical-analysis/use-volatility-as-indicator-in-futures-trading/), while new moons are sometimes associated with calm. Although this concept is more speculative than scientific, some traders incorporate lunar cycles into their strategies, blending traditional technical analysis with astrological insights.

### Influence of celestial phenomena on human behavior

While the scientific connection between celestial cycles and market movements remains debated, integrating these ideas into trading strategies can offer a unique perspective on market cycles and trends.

The theorized impact of celestial cycles on futures markets could be attributed to the broader influence of celestial phenomena on human behavior. Sun and moon cycles might subtly affect collective moods, [risk](/futures/futures-trading-basics/risk-management/) tolerance, and decision-making processes, leading to market shifts.

During periods of increased solar activity, some believe there could be heightened market enthusiasm and/or anxiety, influencing trading volumes and price movements. Similarly, lunar phases might align with fluctuations in investor confidence, thereby affecting market trends.

### Impact of non-traditional factors on trading strategies

While the influence of celestial cycles on futures markets remains speculative and unconventional, it offers an intriguing perspective for those looking to incorporate non-traditional factors into their trading strategies. By exploring these cycles, traders may gain a broader understanding of market psychology and identify unique patterns that others might overlook.

Whether used as a primary strategy or a supplementary tool, understanding celestial cycles could provide a new viewpoint when navigating the complexities of futures trading.
