# When Are My Funds Available? Understanding Deposit Timeframes in the NinjaTrader Platform

By
NinjaTrader Team

April 24, 2025

If you're ready to fund your NinjaTrader account and start trading futures, understanding how long it takes for your funds to clear is an important first step. Whether you're gearing up to place your first trade or looking to respond quickly to market opportunities, knowing the ins and outs of available funding methods can help you get started with confidence.

Many traders ask whether they should fund their NinjaTrader account via ACH, debit card (including Apple Pay/Google Pay), or wire transfer. In this guide, we’ll walk through each of these methods, explain how long each typically takes, and help you choose the best option based on your needs.

## Key differences in deposit types

Whether you’re funding your account for the first time or looking for the quickest way to react to market movement, NinjaTrader gives you flexible, secure, and reliable options to keep your trading on track. ACH transfers, debit card deposits, and wire transfers are all popular options, but they differ in processing time and use cases:

| Feature | ACH | Debit card | Digital Wallet (Apple Pay/Google Pay) | Wire transfers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Processing** **Speed** | Average 1 day for processing | Average 1 day for processing | Average 1 day for processing | Few hours to 1 day |
| **Deposit limit** | Up to $5,000 per transaction | Up to $5,000 per transaction | Up to $5,000 per transaction | No limit |
| **Cutoff time** | 1:30 pm CT | 6:00 pm CT | 6:00 pm CT | Posted upon receipt |
| **Best for** | Standard bank funding | Quick, manual deposits | Fast mobile deposits | Large or urgent deposits |
| **Notes** | Timing may vary by bank; subject to a $5.00 minimum | Subject to card issuer approval; subject to a $5.00 minimum | Uses linked debit card | Timing depends on sending bank |

*Debit card deposits submitted by 6:00 PM Central will be available for trading by 7:00 AM Central after 1 business day of processing. For example, funds placed before 6:00 PM on Monday will be available for trading by 7:00 AM on Wednesday after 1 business day of processing on Tuesday.   
  
Please note that this feature is available only for Individual Brokerage accounts, and only one debit card can be linked to your account at a time.*

#### 

#### 1. ACH transfers

Automated clearing house (ACH) funding is a secure and widely used method for transferring funds directly from your U.S. bank account to your NinjaTrader account. Here’s what you should know about ACH transfers:

* Funds are, on average, available in one processing  day after the transaction is initiated when verified by Plaid, NinjaTrader’s third-party partner for ACH transfers.
* ACH deposits requested before 1:30  pm CT  are processed the same day and are typically posted to your account overnight. Transfers may not settle on weekends or banking holidays, which can affect processing time.
* Once your bank account is verified, you can initiate transfers directly in the NinjaTrader platform. ACH transfers are subject to a $5.00 minimum.
* There are no fees for ACH transfers to NinjaTrader.
* International users may fund via ACH if they have a U.S. bank account.

---

***Pro tip:** To minimize delays, initiate ACH transfers early in the day and during the workweek.*

---

#### 2. Debit card deposits

Debit card funding is a fast and convenient way to deposit funds into your NinjaTrader account, making it ideal for traders who want quick access to the markets.

* Funds typically take one business day to process.
* Deposits requested before 6:00 pm CT  will be processed that evening. Transactions made on weekends or during market holidays will be processed the next business day. Debit card transfers are subject to a $5.00 minimum.
* There are no fees for debit card deposits to NinjaTrader.
* The maximum debit card deposit is $5,000. You may need to wait for your funds to post before you can initiate another deposit.

Debit card deposits require one business day of processing before becoming available for trading. Deposits submitted before 6:00 pm CT will generally be available by 7:00 am CT on the second business day.

Example: A deposit submitted before 6:00 pm CT on Monday will be processed Monday evening, complete one full business day of processing on Tuesday, and be available for trading by 7:00 am CT on Wednesday.

---

***Pro tip:** To help ensure your funds are processed the same evening, submit your debit card deposit request before 6:00 pm CT on a regular business day. To help avoid delays, plan your deposit around weekends and market holidays.*

---

#### 3. **Digital wallet deposits (Apple Pay/Google Pay)**

Digital wallet funding (Apple Pay or Google Pay) is a fast and convenient way to deposit funds into your NinjaTrader account using a linked debit card, making it ideal for traders who want a streamlined mobile funding experience.

* Funds typically take one business day to process.
* Deposits requested before 6:00 pm CT will be processed that evening. Transactions made on weekends or during market holidays will be processed the next business day.
* There are no fees for digital wallet deposits to NinjaTrader.
* The maximum digital wallet deposit is $5,000. You may need to wait for your funds to post before you can initiate another deposit.

**Important:**Digital wallet deposits use debit card funding only. Credit cards, gift cards, and buy now, pay later (BNPL) methods are not supported.

Digital wallet deposits require one business day of processing before becoming available for trading. Deposits submitted before 6:00 pm CT will generally be available by 7:00 am CT on the second business day.

Example: A deposit submitted before 6:00 pm CT on Monday will be processed Monday evening, complete one full business day of processing on Tuesday, and be available for trading by 7:00 am CT on Wednesday.

---

***Pro tip****: To help ensure your funds are processed the same evening, submit your digital wallet deposit request before 6:00 pm CT on a regular business day. To help avoid delays, plan your deposit around weekends and market holidays.*

---

#### 4. Wire transfers

Wire transfers are a reliable funding method often used for larger deposits or when sending funds from outside the [NinjaTrader platform](/trading-platform/) interface. This method is best suited for high-value transfers or for traders who prefer to work directly through their financial institution.

* For domestic wire transfers, funds are generally posted to your account  within a few hours to the next business day once received by NinjaTrader’s clearing firm. However, the speed of delivery depends on when your bank initiates the wire and its internal processing times.
* International wire transfers can take 1-2 business days to be received.
* NinjaTrader does not charge a fee for inbound wire transfers, but your bank may charge a fee for sending a wire transfer. Please contact your bank for additional information on their fees.
* Wire transfers are initiated through your bank and require accurate delivery instructions. You can access NinjaTrader’s wire details by logging into your account dashboard and navigating to the Funding section.
* Wire transfers do not have a cutoff time; they are processed and posted to your account when received.

---

***Pro tips:** Always double-check your wire details to make sure your bank account title matches your brokerage account title. Allow time for your bank’s processing when scheduling a transfer, especially before weekends or holidays.*

## How do I fund my account in the NinjaTrader platform?

To deposit funds into your account:

1. Log in to the NinjaTrader platform (mobile or desktop).
2. Go to the **Account** section.
3. Select Transfer, then choose **Fund.**
4. Choose your method: **ACH Deposit, Debit Card, Digital Wallet,**or**Wire Transfer**.
5. Follow the prompts to complete your deposit.

All deposits are handled through secure, regulated payment providers to ensure your funds and personal information remain protected.

## Tips for a smooth funding experience

* **Plan ahead for weekends and holidays.** Deposits made outside of regular banking hours will be delayed until the next business day.
* **Monitor your email.** You’ll receive a confirmation once your deposit is received and another once it’s been posted to your account.
* **Have your bank info ready.**If you’re using ACH, make sure your bank account is linked and verified in advance.
