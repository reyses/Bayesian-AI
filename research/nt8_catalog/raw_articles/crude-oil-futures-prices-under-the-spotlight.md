# Crude Oil Futures Prices Under the Spotlight

By
NinjaTrader Team

September 28, 2023

Crude oil is increasingly in focus as the policy decisions of interested governments and broader coalitions drive crude oil prices out of the $65 - $83 trading range seen for most of the year. While the crude oil bulls are happy, higher crude oil prices result in higher gas prices and could have an exacerbating effect on inflation.

Bob Iaccino joined this week’s “See the Futures” to discuss why the impact of crude oil prices on inflation could be the largest influence on the Fed’s rate decision well into 2024, and how the outlook on crude oil price influences the CME’s FedWatch Probability Matrix through 2024.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Current and historical refinery capacity
* Possibility of another rate hike before year-end
* How energy prices affect two kinds of inflation
* What could cause oil prices to stabilize
* Chart analysis of crude oil, heating oil, and E-mini S&P 500 futures
