# 6 Steps to Build an Effective Futures Trading Chart Layout

By
NinjaTrader Team

June 12, 2025

A well-structured chart layout isn’t about having the most indicators—it’s about building a workspace that helps you read the markets with confidence and consistency. Whether you’re new to futures or refining an existing strategy, having the right customized chart layout can give you a clearer picture of market behavior and help you identify better trade opportunities.

Pro trader Merritt Black shared tips on how to design a layout that supports smart, informed trading.

## Step 1: Establish market context with higher time frames

Every effective futures trading chart layout starts with understanding the bigger picture. Using daily or weekly charts, you can assess where current price action sits in relation to recent value areas. This high-level view can help shape your directional bias and prepare you for intraday setups.

#### Key tools:

* **Volume-weighted average price (**[**VWAP**](/futures/blogs/what-is-volume-weighted-average-price-vwap/)**):**This anchors price to value over a given period.
* **Standard deviation bands:** These show how far price has moved from VWAP and help assess strength or exhaustion.
* **Weekly profiles:** These can help you visualize where value was built during previous sessions.

Starting with context allows you to trade with purpose instead of reacting to short-term noise.

## Step 2: Use VWAP for structure and momentum

VWAP is a cornerstone of many trading strategies because it reflects the average traded price weighted by volume. When paired with standard deviation bands (typically ±1 and ±2), it forms a dynamic framework for understanding price behavior. H4: Why VWAP matters:

* In balanced markets, price tends to revert toward VWAP.
* In trending markets, price may ride the outer deviation bands.
* The further price moves from VWAP, the more energy it typically takes to continue.

VWAP doesn’t generate trade signals on its own—but it does help identify trade locations, gauge momentum, and frame risk.

## Step 3: Add volume profile to identify value areas

When creating your futures trading chart layout, be sure to add [volume profile](/futures/blogs/how-to-use-volume-profile-and-the-importance-of-volume-profile-data/) charts to help you map out where trading activity has occurred by price level. This can help reveal where the market sees value and where price has been accepted or rejected.

#### Use volume profile to:

* Spot high-volume nodes (acceptance areas)
* Identify low-volume zones (potential turning points)
* Compare current price to prior value areas

You can apply profiles to different time frames—daily, weekly, or session-based—to track how value is shifting. This can give you a more detailed picture of the auction process and potential inflection points.

## Step 4: Zoom in with order flow

Once you’ve identified a key level or area of interest, [order flow tools](/futures/blogs/the-power-of-ninjatrader-order-flow-tools/) can help you fine-tune entries. Cumulative delta, for example, shows the net difference between aggressive buying and selling, offering insight into who’s currently in control.

#### Order flow can help you:

* Confirm breakout or reversal setups
* Monitor market strength or weakness
* Avoid entries in thin or uncommitted moves

By combining order flow with your VWAP and volume profile levels, you can improve timing and manage risk more precisely.

## Step 5: Focus on the edges, not the middle

In both VWAP and volume profile structures, the edges often offer the best trade setups. The center—like the point of control or VWAP itself—is where price tends to consolidate, often leading to choppy action.

#### Why edges matter:

* They define where price transitions from acceptance to rejection.
* They provide cleaner entry points with more defined risk.
* They often act as boundaries for breakouts or fades.

Build your futures trading chart layout to clearly highlight these areas so you can recognize and react to opportunities faster.

## Step 6: Align your tools with a clear market narrative

An effective chart layout doesn’t just show you what’s happening—it helps you understand *why* it’s happening. Every tool in your setup should contribute to your read on market context, structure, and order flow.

#### Ask yourself:

* Are we in trend or mean reversion mode?
* Is the market accepting or rejecting recent value?
* What is order flow telling me at this level?
* Does the setup align with the broader picture?

When everything lines up—higher time frame context, structure, and execution—you’re trading with a clear narrative and a greater sense of control.

## Build the chart layout that works for you

Your futures trading chart layout should support your decision-making, not complicate it. Focus on a few powerful tools that complement each other:

* Higher time frame charts for directional context
* VWAP and deviation bands for structure
* Volume profiles for trade location
* Order flow for timing and confirmation
