# Focus on Japanese Yen with Dan Gramza

By
NinjaTrader Team

August 03, 2023

Major global central bank activities prevail as post-Covid inflationary pressures continue to weigh on the futures markets. One country has been bucking the tight money trend: Japan. The third largest country as measured by GDP is marching to their own drummer. Will this trend continue?  
  
Special guest Dan Gramza, long-term futures trading professional and head of Gramza Capital Management, joined this week’s episode of ‘See the Futures’ where the focus was on the Bank of Japan and possible ramifications to the CME Group’s Yen/USD forex futures contract.  
  
  
  
**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Difference between the Federal Reserve’s and Bank of Japan’s approaches to messaging
* Effects of central bank policy on the markets vs. the economy
* Changing demographics to a country’s monetary policy
* The carry trade, and the risks it may contain
* Chart analysis of Japanese Yen futures
