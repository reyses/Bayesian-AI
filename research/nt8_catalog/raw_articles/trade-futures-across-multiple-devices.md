## Best practices for managing futures trading on multiple devices

Trading on the go is powerful, but staying organized is key. Here are a few tips to help keep everything running smoothly:

* **Stick to one login per user** to prevent order conflicts.
* **Double-check trades** before switching platforms.
* **Enable mobile alerts** so you never miss a fill.
* **Use the Account Dashboard** to track orders and balances.
* **Log out of unused sessions** when you’re done trading.

When you’re trading across multiple devices, a little organization can go a long way toward keeping your trades clean and helping reduce operational errors.

## Common mistakes to avoid when trading across devices

No one’s perfect; even seasoned traders can run into issues. Here’s what to watch for:

* **Placing duplicate orders** by mistake on separate devices
* **Leaving sessions open**on shared or public computers
* **Assuming chart layouts are shared** (each platform has its own view)
* **Using outdated app versions** (keep the app updated for the best experience)
