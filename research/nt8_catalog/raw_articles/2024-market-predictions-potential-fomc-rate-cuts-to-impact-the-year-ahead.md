# 2024 Market Predictions: Potential FOMC Rate Cuts to Impact the Year Ahead

By
NinjaTrader Team

January 04, 2024

In 2024, the big influencer in all markets could hinge on if/when the FOMC cuts rates. TJM Institutional Services Managing Director Jim Iuorio joined us on this week’s episode of “See the Futures” to discuss some of the major drivers for several futures markets for the coming year.

**Additional topics discussed in this free livestream:**

* Relationship of dollar weakness and flight to safety in [gold](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/metals/gold/)
* Low participation in longer-end treasury auctions
* Why the job market may not be as healthy as it seems
* [Bitcoin](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/cryptocurrency/bitcoin/)’s bullishness in 2023
* Chart analysis of [E-mini S&P 500 Index](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/index/e-mini-s-p-500/), [E-mini Dow Jones Index](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/futures-contracts/index/e-mini-dow/), gold, Euro FX, Bitcoin, and 10-year Note futures
* Comparison chart of 2-year, 10-year and 30-year Yield futures
